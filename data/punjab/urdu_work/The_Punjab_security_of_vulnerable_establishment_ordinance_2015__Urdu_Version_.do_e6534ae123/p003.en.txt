3- Security Advisory Committee:- (1) The District Coordination Officer shall constitute a Security Advisory Committee in the sub-division, which shall consist of the Sub-Divisional Police Officer, [unclear] and the following members:-

(a) An officer of the grade of Scale [unclear] or above of the concerned administration;
(b) An officer of the grade of Scale [unclear] or above of the Police Special Branch;
(c) An officer of the grade of Scale [unclear] or above of the counter-terrorism agency; and
(d) Main nominees of traders, holders and other stakeholders.

(2) Each member of the Security Advisory Committee shall remain in office for a period of one year, but the District Coordination Officer may re-nominate them for the same period.

(3) Any member of the Security Advisory Committee may resign from their office to the District Coordination Officer, and the District Coordination Officer may nominate another member in their place for the remaining period.

(4) No act or proceeding of the Committee shall be declared void on the ground of mere vacancy of any seat or any defect in the constitution of the Committee.

(5) The meetings of the Committee shall be held at least once at such time and place as the Chairperson may decide.

(6) The Committee shall adopt such procedure for the conduct of proceedings in its meetings as it may deem fit, or otherwise, if it is not explicitly stated, the procedure decided by the Chairperson shall be followed.
