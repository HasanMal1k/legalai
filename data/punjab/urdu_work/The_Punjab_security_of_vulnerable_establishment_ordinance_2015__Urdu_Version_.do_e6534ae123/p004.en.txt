4- Duties of the Commission:- (1) The Commission:
(a) shall identify and recommend institutions as declared vulnerable institutions;
(b) shall inspect vulnerable institutions on an annual basis;
(c) shall issue written advisories for such security arrangements, where necessary, based on the threat to vulnerable institutions arising from the location of the institution, the defensive force stationed at the institution, or other factors;
(d) shall send a report to the head of the District Police in case of non-compliance with any advisory.
(2) The Commission may constitute a sub-commission and may delegate the duty of inspecting vulnerable institutions on an annual basis to such sub-commission.

5- Vulnerable Institutions:- (1) The Commission shall identify any institution as a vulnerable institution by notification and shall inform the District Coordination Officer.
(2) The District Coordination Officer, by notification, may declare any institution a vulnerable institution. He shall exercise the powers of vulnerable institutions and shall issue annual [unclear] lists.
(3) The District Coordination Officer, by notification, may, on the recommendation of the Commission or otherwise, remove any vulnerable institution from the list of vulnerable institutions.
(4) The District Coordination Officer shall provide copies of the list of vulnerable institutions and the monitored list to the Government, the head of the District Police, and all [unclear] commissioners of the district.

6- Advisories by the Commission:- (1) The Commission shall issue advisories for the threat to any such vulnerable institution and shall determine the time within which the threat shall be mitigated, and shall make necessary security arrangements.
