16- Settlement of Offence :- (1) Subject to sub-section (2), the Government or an authorized officer of the Government may, at any stage, settle an offence by way of exceptional settlement under this Ordinance, provided that it is less than fifty thousand rupees.
(2) If an offence has already been punished under this Ordinance or if the offence has been settled by the Government or any authorized officer thereof, then the offence shall not be settleable under this Ordinance.

17- Power to Hear Excuses :- No court shall hear any suit or application against any proceeding, direction, or order issued under this Ordinance.

18- In Addition to Other Laws :- The provisions of this Ordinance shall be in addition to any other law and shall not be in conflict with it.

19- Power to Make Rules :- The Government may, by notification in the official Gazette, make rules for the implementation of the purposes of this Ordinance.

20- Repeal and Saving :- (1) The Shops and Establishments (Security) Act, Punjab 1999 (Chapter 2) of 1999 is hereby repealed.
(2) Notwithstanding the repeal of the Shops and Establishments (Security) Act, Punjab 1999 (Chapter 2) of 1999, any action taken under the repealed Act shall be as effective as any action taken under this Ordinance.
