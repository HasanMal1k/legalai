12- Appeal:— (1) The manager of an insecure institution may appeal against the advice, warning, or direction to close or stop the work of such institution within three days of the receipt of the advice, warning, or direction to close or stop the work of such institution to the District Intelligence Committee, as nominated by the Government.

(2) The District Intelligence Committee, after providing an opportunity for a personal hearing to the appellant and the representative of the Commission, shall pass an appeal order as may be necessary, and the appeal order shall be final.

(3) The District Intelligence Committee shall pass the appeal order in writing, but shall not take more than seven days of time, and shall immediately inform the appellant and the Commission of its decision.

(4) If the manager of any insecure institution has obtained the permission of the District Intelligence Committee within the stipulated time, he shall act in accordance with the advice, warning, or order of the Commission in the order of the District Intelligence Committee.

13- Manager shall provide evidence:— The manager of any insecure institution shall provide evidence of any offense, if obtained from the sources of the secretary of the insecure institution, to the police or any investigation agency.

14- Punishment:— If any person knowingly violates the provisions of section 10, or fails to act in accordance with the directions issued under sections 11 and 12, or fails to comply with the undertaking given under section 11, or fails to provide evidence to the investigation agency under section 13, he shall be liable to imprisonment for a term which may extend to six months and a fine which may extend from fifty thousand to one lakh rupees.

15- Bailable and Non-Bailable Offenses:— (1) Any offense under the Ordinance shall be a bailable and non-bailable offense.

(2) The manager of the first class shall try the offense under the provisions of Chapter 22 of the Code relating to summary trial under the Ordinance.
