7- Security of Unsecured Establishments:- The meter of an unsecured establishment shall make proper and satisfactory security arrangements for the protection of the unsecured establishment and shall comply with the Committee's advance within the stipulated time in the advance.

8- Security of Public Places:- The local government or any other local authority shall ensure the cleaning of roads, streets, and construction sites, shall arrange for the removal of waste heaps on a daily basis, and shall provide for the construction of new pipelines and water supply lines so that no hazardous material is transported to public places.

9- SEO shall Inspect:- The SEO may inspect any unsecured establishment at any time and, informing the head of the district police, may send a final report to the Chairman of the Committee.

10- Warning:- (1) If the Committee is convinced on the basis of information received from any source or the inspection report of the SEO that an unsecured establishment has not made the security arrangements as per the Committee's advance, the Committee may issue a written warning to the meter of the unsecured establishment explicitly stating the violation of the instructions contained in the advance.

(2) The meter of the unsecured establishment shall comply with the Committee's advance within the time mentioned in the warning letter.

11- Closure of Unsecured Establishment:- (1) If the meter of an unsecured establishment does not comply with the Committee's advance within the stipulated time, the Committee may issue an order to completely or partially close the unsecured establishment or may restrain it from carrying out work until the technical requirements of the advance are fully complied with and security arrangements are made, or the meter may agree in writing to remove [it] within the stipulated time with the permission of the Committee.
