Whereas it is expedient that measures be taken for the security of non-secure institutions, such as the prevention of incidents of targeted terrorism and other crimes, and that evidence be obtained through the use of modern equipment for the investigation and prevention of crimes, and matters related thereto be dealt with;

And whereas the Provincial Assembly of Punjab is not in session and the Governor of Punjab is informed that circumstances demand that immediate action be taken;

Therefore, exercising the powers conferred upon them under Article 128, sub-section (1) of the Constitution of the Islamic Republic of Pakistan, the Governor of Punjab hereby enacts and enforces the following Ordinance:-

1- Short title, extent, and commencement:- (1) This Ordinance shall be called the Non-Secure Institutions Security Ordinance, Punjab 2015.
(2) It shall apply to the whole of Punjab.
(3) It shall come into force immediately.

2- Definitions:- In this Ordinance:
(a) "Code" means the Code of Criminal Procedure, 1898 (Act of 1898);
(b) "Committee" means the Security Advisory Committee to function under Section 3 of this Ordinance;
(c) "District Coordination Officer" means the District Coordination Officer of the district;
(d) "Government" means the Government of Punjab;
(e) "Head of District Police" means the Commissioner, Senior Police Officer, or District Police Officer;
(f) "Chief" means the owner or occupier of a non-secure institution, and includes any person to whom the owner or occupier has appointed or authorized for the management of the non-secure institution;
(g) "Schedule" means as expressly provided in the rules made under this Ordinance;
