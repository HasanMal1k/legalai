3- Prohibition on the use of sound systems :- (1) No person shall be prohibited under this Ordinance from using their own sound system, assisting in its use, or permitting its use, provided that it does not cause any loud, unnecessary, or offensive noise, or such sounds that cause distress, nuisance, or discomfort to persons in the vicinity, or interfere with their rest, peace, health, tranquility, or safety.
(2) Notwithstanding anything contained in any other law of this Ordinance, no person shall use a sound system in a manner that violates the prescribed limits.
(3) The Government shall determine the limits of sound systems by means of notification.

4- Regulated use of sound systems :- (1) Subject to the following sub-section (2), no person shall cause the operation or use of a sound system:
(a) In a manner that creates noise or causes noise in a public place or in a manner that causes discomfort and nuisance to the general public in the vicinity; or
(b) In any place in the vicinity :-
(i) During prayer in a place of worship;
(ii) In a hospital providing facilities for patients at any time of the day or night;
(iii) In educational institutions, courts, hospitals providing facilities for patients, or any other residential area, office, or place of business during working hours, in such a volume or in such a manner that the use of the sound system disrupts the routine of work or occupation;
(iv) In a house or any other place used for human habitation at any time, unless expressly stated; or
(v) In any place of worship in such a manner and at such a volume that the sound emanating from the sound system can be heard at a distance of ten times the boundaries of that place of worship; or
(vi) Broadcasting from any public place or other contrary nature of things, which may cause a sense of fear in society, and such things are heard from outside the boundaries of those places or from a distance or can be heard by humans.
