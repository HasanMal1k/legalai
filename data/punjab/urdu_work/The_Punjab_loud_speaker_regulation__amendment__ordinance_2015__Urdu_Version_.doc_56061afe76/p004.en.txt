(2) Under sub-section (2) of section 3, any person:
(a) may use an external sound system in any place of worship only temporarily on the occasion of Adhan, Friday, or Eid prayers, at the same rates as for the announcement of any ceremony, any person, or a day of mourning, or
(b) may use an external sound system at any public place for a reasonable duration with the legal permission of the Government or an officer nominated by the Government, and the use of such sound system shall be subject to the conditions specified in the permission letter.

5- Inspection:- In order to ensure compliance with the provisions of the Ordinance, the Station House Officer (SHO) of the local area shall regularly inspect the sound system of every place of worship at prescribed regular intervals, shall conduct such inspections, and shall complete the record of such inspections in the prescribed manner.

6- Punishment:- Any person violating any clause of section 3 or 4 shall be liable to imprisonment for up to 6 months and a fine ranging from 25 thousand to one lakh rupees.

7- Power of Seizure:- (1) The police officer in charge of the sub-division, who is not below the rank of Sub-Inspector, may seize any sound system which has been used in commission of an offense under this Ordinance or on which there is a reasonable suspicion of being used in an offense based on the relevant incident.
(2) Any sound system seized under this section, without any delay, shall be produced before the competent court for the trial of the offense under this Ordinance.

8- Offense Bailable by Police and Non-Bailable:- (1) Any offense committed under this Ordinance shall be bailable by police and non-bailable.
(2) The offense shall be trialed as per the provisions relating to non-bailable offenses mentioned in Chapter 22 of the Code of Criminal Procedure for offenses committed under this Ordinance.

9- Composition of Offense:- (1) Subject to sub-section (2), the Government or any person specially authorized by the Government may compose any offense under this Ordinance at any level, provided it is an administrative offense and the fine is not less than five thousand rupees, subject to the condition of prevention.
