Whereas it is expedient that for the purpose of preventing the spread of public mischief and creating unrest in society, and to curb the activities of anti-social elements, public property, public peace, and the interests of the people, it is necessary to declare the use of certain sound systems in the Province as prohibited and illegal and to take necessary action in this regard;

And whereas the Punjab Assembly is not in session and the Governor of Punjab is satisfied that circumstances are such that immediate action is required;

Therefore, the Governor of Punjab, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him under Article 128, sub-section (1) of the Constitution of the Islamic Republic of Pakistan, hereby makes and enforces the following Ordinance:-

1- Short Title, Scope and Commencement:-
(1) This Ordinance shall be called the Sound System (Regulation) Ordinance, Punjab 2015.
(2) It shall apply to the whole of Punjab.
(3) It shall come into force immediately.

2- Definitions:- In this Ordinance:-
(a) "Area" means the United Provinces of India 1898 (5th Act 1898);
(b) "Government" means the Government of Punjab;
(c) "Place of Worship" means a mosque, Imambargah, dargah, temple, or any other place of worship of any sect or religion;
(d) "Prescribed" means as prescribed by means of rules made under this Ordinance;
(e) "Public Place" means a public street, public highway, public park, or playground, or any other place to which members of the public have access by invitation or without invitation;
(f) "Sound System" means a loud speaker, sound system, or any other place as may be specified; and
(g) "Proximity of Wall" means within a distance of two kilometers, that area or place where a sound system is placed.
