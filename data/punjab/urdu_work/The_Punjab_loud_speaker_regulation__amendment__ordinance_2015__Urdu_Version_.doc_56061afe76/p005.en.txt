10- Prohibition: The courts hearing offenses under the Ordinance may issue orders regarding any loudspeaker, sound system, papa, or shanbalgi used in the commission of an offense under the Ordinance.

11- Power to make rules: The Government may, by notification in the official gazette, make rules for the implementation of the objectives of the Ordinance.

12- Compliance and Exemption: (1) Any act prohibited under the Loudspeaker and Sound System (Papa) Regulation Punjab 1965 (2nd Amendment 1965) shall be prohibited.
(2) Notwithstanding compliance with the Loudspeaker and Sound System (Papa) Regulation Punjab 1965 (2nd Amendment 1965), any order or any act of violence committed under the prohibited Ordinance shall be prohibited, or any order or any act of violence committed under the Ordinance.
