1.6.1 The amount of the approved grant for ongoing expenses shall not be spent on fuel for vehicles or on their repair.

1.7 Financial assistance shall be spent only for the purposes mentioned in the project approved by the District Commissioner / Engineering Commissioner / Council. In case of violation, in addition to the unspent financial assistance amount, the Commissioner / Council shall issue a one-month notice for the recovery of the spent amount to the institution, and in case of non-payment, necessary legal action shall be taken against the welfare institution. If the Council deems it appropriate, it may recommend to the relevant registration authority to suspend / debar the concerned institution.

1.8.1 Needy NGOs shall submit an application on the prescribed form to the relevant District Commissioner for financial assistance up to Rs. 50,000/-. The District Bait-ul-Mal Commissioner may, after completing all required formalities, approve financial assistance up to Rs. 50,000/- under the principles of the Managing Grant from 6% of the funds available with it, and for this, the NGO shall have to deposit 1/3 of the assistance amount from its own resources.

1.8.2 The District Commissioner may also attempt to fulfill / waive the condition of the assistance granted to any NGO before or after the approval of the assistance; the Council shall have the authority under Clause 42 to approve or waive any recommended condition. However, for assistance exceeding Rs. 50,000/-, the NGO seeking assistance shall also submit an application to the District Commissioner.
