(9) The Council shall constitute permanent administrative and financial committees for the management of administrative and financial affairs.
(10) In addition to the permanent administrative and financial committees, the Council may constitute committees consisting of at least two members other than the Secretary of the Council for the discharge of its functions or for a special purpose.
(11) The Council may delegate all its powers or any powers acquired under the Bait-ul-Mal Act 1991 to any of its members, either unconditionally or on specific terms and conditions.
(12) It shall be authorized to appoint a Chartered Accountant for the annual audit of its accounts, who shall be in accordance with the definition of a Chartered Accountant as provided in the Chartered Accountants Ordinance 1961.
(13) The Council may purchase movable/immovable property and, in case of necessity, may transfer such property to any other person or institution in any legal manner.
(14) It shall provide the auditor with all account books, documents, explanations, and information requested by the auditor for the purposes of the audit.
(15) Upon receipt of a complaint against the Chairman of any District Committee for corruption, misconduct, irregularity, negligence of duty, or non-performance, the Council may conduct an inquiry through the Commission and, through Bait-ul-Mal, shall get the inquiry conducted; and upon the charges being proved, it shall be authorized to remove the concerned Chairman/member from the post, and its decision shall be final and shall not be challenged in any court.

- 5 Head Office of the Council
The Head Office of the Council shall be located in Lahore.

- 6 Meetings of the Council
(1) There shall be at least one meeting of the Council every four months.
