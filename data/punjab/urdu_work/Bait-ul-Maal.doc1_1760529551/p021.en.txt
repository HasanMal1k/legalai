9- A member shall give oral or written notice of their absence to the Deputy Administrator or Secretary of the Council from any meeting of the Council.

10- "I" shall be authorized to recommend the nomination of the Chairman of any district to the Executive Committee of the Council, and the Executive Committee shall issue a notification for the nomination in any district council and its authentication shall be done in the next meeting of the Council.

11- "I" shall be authorized to issue the notification of the nomination of the Chairman of the District Committee, members of the District Committee, and the Vice Chairman, after consultation with the concerned member of the Council and the concerned District Committee, orally / in writing or via public phone. This notification shall be presented in the next meeting of the Council for information.

12- On the basis of a complaint from any District Committee or other sources, "I" shall be required to report in the meeting, with the consultation of the concerned member of the Council, regarding the continuous absence without notice from three consecutive meetings of the District Committee of any member of the District Committee, lack of interest in the work of the Committee, or irresponsible behavior. If "I" deem it appropriate, "I" shall be authorized to terminate (denotify) the membership of the concerned member of the District Committee on my own discretion with the consultation of the concerned member of the Council; however, information of this shall be given in the next meeting of the Council. If "I" deem it appropriate, before terminating the membership of the concerned member, "I" shall be authorized to issue a show-cause notice to them on my own discretion, and in case of an unsatisfactory reply or no reply within the stipulated time, "I" shall be authorized to terminate their membership.

13- In case of the submission of the current resignation by any member of the District Committee / Chairman / Vice Chairman, "I" shall be authorized to approve the resignation on my own discretion and issue a notification of the termination of their membership (denotification); information of this shall be given in the next meeting of the Council.

14- On the complaint of any irregularities, lack of interest, and corruption from the Chairman / Vice Chairman of the District Committee, "I" shall be authorized to immediately suspend them or remove them from work. In case of a complaint against the Chairman, the Committee or Council shall appoint an acting Chairman, and in case of the apology of the Vice Chairman, "I" shall appoint any member of the Committee as Chairman. In case of a complaint against the Vice Chairman, "I" shall be able to appoint any member of the Committee as Vice Chairman. "I" shall be authorized to appoint the Chairman / Vice Chairman as per the rules mentioned in the nomination by "I".
