In the light of Islamic teachings and the *Sunnah* (exemplary conduct), the helpless, the elderly, the disabled, and the underprivileged are as respectable as others; this is the demand of justice and brotherhood. Leaving these underprivileged individuals to the mercy of circumstances regarding their inevitable compulsions and difficulties is contrary to moral values and religious traditions. Therefore, to fulfill the *Fiqh* (jurisprudential) needs of such individuals suffering from financial and social issues to a certain extent, uniform financial assistance teams have been initiated from the *Bait-ul-Mal* for them.

2.1 The funds available with the District Bait-ul-Mal Committee are limited, and in view of the day-to-day increase in the number of applicants, assistance to all applicants is not possible. In the second category, assistance is generally provided on a first-come, first-served basis. However, the nature of assistance in this category is slightly different; i.e., priority shall be given to providing immediate assistance to affected individuals who have lost their livelihood due to natural disasters, theft, death, disability, or the burning of their sources of income. Therefore, if the policy of assisting the poorest of the poor is in operation, it is mandatory for the District Committee to determine the criteria of eligibility and, while receiving applications, the Secretary of the Committee/responsible person must ensure that the application is complete in every respect and that it has been submitted for their own basic needs. If the details are mentioned in the application, otherwise, the required information shall be completed by the applicant.

2.2 The District Committee shall formulate a system or procedure under which the standing score of the qualifications of each applicant shall be determined by the Secretary Committee through the District Committee's meeting ten days prior to the initial screening process, after which all applications shall be scrutinized by the concerned members of the Committee, and the approval of assistance and issuance of checks shall be done on a first-come, first-served basis within three months of the date of receipt of the application.
