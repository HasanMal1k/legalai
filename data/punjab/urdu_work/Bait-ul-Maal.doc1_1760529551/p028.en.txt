27. District Bait-ul-Mal Committees
Guidelines:-
1- The membership of the District Committee should be in accordance with the size and composition of the district population, i.e., for a population of approximately 2 lakh people, at least one member should be elected.
2- At least two members should be taken from each tehsil.
3- In those districts where there is a significant population of minorities, they should also be given appropriate representation in the District Bait-ul-Mal Committee.
4- At least two women should be given representation in the district.
5- In low-population districts, the number of members should be kept at a minimum of 10.
6- Active NPOs (Non-Profit Organizations) should also be given appropriate representation in each district and a member of the committee should be nominated as the Chair of the District Coordination Council (DCC).
7- While selecting members, the following points should be kept in mind:
a. He should be a permanent resident of the district, i.e., a healthy, courageous, and sensible personality of Pakistani nationality.
b. He should be of firm character and be known as a respectable citizen in his own area.
c. He should have an interest in social welfare works and be known as an active social worker in his respective district/area to the extent possible.
d. Such social workers who avoid party politics and sectarianism and are committed to doing good only for the pleasure of God, and who are willing to reach the rightful beneficiaries with the Bait-ul-Mal funds with full sincerity and are inclined to spend their time and money for this purpose; and who possess a prominent position and performance in education, health, or social welfare works.
