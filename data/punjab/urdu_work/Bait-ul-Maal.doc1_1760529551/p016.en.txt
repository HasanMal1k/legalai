(6) In the absence of the "Trustee", the Secretary of the Social Welfare Department shall preside over the meeting; provided that in the absence of the Secretary of Social Welfare as well, the members present in the meeting shall elect any one of themselves to preside over the meeting.

7- Procedure of the Council's Proceedings

(1) The quorum of the meeting shall consist of 7 members including the "Trustee". The quorum of an emergency meeting shall consist of 4 members including the "Trustee", in which the presence of at least two non-official members shall be mandatory.
(2) A decision of the Council shall be deemed to be made in accordance with the majority of the members present and voting in the meeting.
(3) In respect of any matter which has been discussed in the meeting and upon which an opinion has been formed, the members may express their views in writing.

8- Views of the Members
If the "Trustee" so desires, he may also request any member to express his views in writing.
