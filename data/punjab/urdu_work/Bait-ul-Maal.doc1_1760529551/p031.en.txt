4- The Secretary of the District Committee shall ensure that monthly/required progress reports are prepared on time from the respective [offices] and forwarded with his signatures by the scheduled date, so that he may remain informed of the latest status of the [budget] allocation.

5- It shall be ensured by the District Committee that every member of the committee performs his duty with full responsibility; otherwise, any instance of indifference, negligence, irresponsibility, or non-cooperation in the committee's work, misconduct, particularly the recommendation of payments on requests without investigation, or attempting to give primary preference to the requests of relatives and acquaintances instead of fulfilling the demands of the [eligible] without justification, shall be reported in writing to the "Admin" so that disciplinary action may be taken against the concerned member.

6- At its own discretion, the District Committee may convene a meeting at appropriate times on any scheduled day within the first ten days of the month within the limits of the district, at a place necessary for deciding the matters of the Bait-ul-Mal Fund. The Chairman shall regularly preside over all its meetings.
