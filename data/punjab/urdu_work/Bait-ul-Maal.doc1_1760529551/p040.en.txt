36- Formation of Sub-Committees
The Chairman shall have the authority to constitute sub-committees as per requirement for specific categories of applications and small committees for NGOs. Each sub-committee shall consist of at least two members.

37- Minutes of the Meeting
The minutes of the meeting shall be recorded entirely in Urdu and shall be preserved in the proceedings register. A copy of the minutes of the meeting shall be sent to all members of the Committee and the Council within a maximum of seven days after the conclusion of the meeting.

38- Accounts
The accounts of the Punjab Bait-ul-Mal Funds and the funds of the District Bait-ul-Mal Committees shall be maintained by the Council in the prescribed form and manner. The record of the accounts of the Punjab Bait-ul-Mal Funds shall be preserved by the Council for the prescribed period and shall be made available in the prescribed manner for inspection and scrutiny by the prescribed persons or agencies.

39- Audit
(i) The Punjab Bait-ul-Mal Council shall appoint auditors to have the annual accounts of the District Committees audited on a simplified basis, such persons as shall be Chartered Accountants within the meaning of the Chartered Accountants Ordinance, 1961.
(ii) The audit by the auditors shall also include the verification of the adequacy and legitimacy of the income and expenditures.
