-4. Members shall endeavor to ensure that received applications are displayed before the convening of the next meeting of the District Committee and shall send the recommendations/reports in a timely manner.

-5. Action shall only be taken on those applications received by the District Committee and which have been assigned a diary number by the District Committee.

-6. In cases of applications from persons having a close relationship or kinship with a member, priority shall be given to other more deserving individuals; failure to do so shall be considered a blemish on the reputation of the members.

-7. All members shall make every effort to collect voluntary aid, donations, alms, and charities from the wealthy individuals of the area and deposit them into the Committee's fund so that the maximum number of deserving individuals may benefit.

-8. Members shall cooperate fully with the Committee and endeavor to act in good faith according to its instructions; and if, for any reason, they are unable to fulfill their responsibilities, they shall respectfully resign from their membership and opt for separation from the Committee.

32- Discipline for Absence and Lack of Interest in District Committee Meetings

-1. If any member of the Committee remains absent without notice or without justification from three consecutive meetings, or exhibits a lack of interest, irresponsible behavior, such as non-cooperation in the Committee's work, misconduct, recommending aid on applications without verification, and attempting to give primary preference to relatives and acquaintances among applicants, then the Chairman of the District Committee may recommend in writing to the 'Amin' of Bait-ul-Mal to terminate the membership of such member.
