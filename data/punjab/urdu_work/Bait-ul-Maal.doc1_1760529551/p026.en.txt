26- Duties and Powers of the Deputy Administrator, Punjab Bait-ul-Mal

1- The "Trustee" shall perform duties in accordance with the instructions and decisions of the Secretary Council / Administrator General, Sayyid Bahood, under the supervision and control of the Punjab Bait-ul-Mal.

2- The Trustee shall be responsible for the performance of the duties of the Head Office of Punjab Bait-ul-Mal and for the supervision of the work of the Staff of the Council; he shall be responsible for appropriate action against any violation of the rules, the preparation of annual reports, and the approval of the agreed share, etc.

3- He shall ensure that monthly performance, income, expenditure, and other required reports are received on time from the District Commissioners, and upon receipt, shall analyze the reports to determine whether the performance, decisions, and distribution of funds, etc., of each Commissioner are in accordance with the Council's policy and rules of procedure.

4- He shall be responsible for continuous contact and necessary correspondence with the Department of Social Welfare and other government departments, the Department of Haj, the Public Works Department, and autonomous bodies regarding the affairs of the Council.

5- Regarding received mail, the "Trustee" shall, in compliance with the oral and written instructions and decisions of the Secretary Council, be bound to keep the Secretary Council informed of any matters of importance without delay.

6- The District Commissioners shall keep the "Trustee" informed from time to time regarding the general performance of the District Bait-ul-Mal; the "Trustee" shall submit actionable proposals regarding the staff, payroll, and appointments, etc., of the Commissioners to the Secretary Council as and when necessary.

7- He shall pursue filed appeals regarding the affairs of Bait-ul-Mal in the courts and shall keep the Secretary Council informed of the progress of each case.
