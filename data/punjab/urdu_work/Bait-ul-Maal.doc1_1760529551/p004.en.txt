Punjab Bait-ul-Mal
Chapter Number 1
Introduction!

(1) The ideological basis of Pakistan is Islam, and the spirit of Islam is social welfare and the well-being of humanity—a complete code of life which provides a lesson for the establishment of a healthy, balanced, and just society through tolerance, brotherhood, affection and kindness, justice and equity in Islamic teachings. Therefore, a concept of an Islamic welfare state that nurtures feelings of self-respect, self-reliance, and God-consciousness in humans, and enables them to live a dignified life free from social and economic problems by protecting them from the light of self-determination and the sense of deprivation through every possible assistance to the poor, helpless, disabled, and orphans, along with the well-being of the general public. The requirement of all this process is extremely important in terms of deep reflection and research, and its practical participation.

(2) For the achievement of this basic objective, Punjab Bait-ul-Mal was established in 1989 through the Bait-ul-Mal Ordinance. As per the Ordinance and subsequently under the Punjab Bait-ul-Mal Act 1991, it attained the status of an autonomous body. Under this Act, a 15-member Bait-ul-Mal Council has been established, whose head is called the "Ameen." This is an honorary post equivalent to a Grade-17 government officer, which is nominated by the Government. The Director General Social Welfare, Development and Bait-ul-Mal, by virtue of his office, performs the duties of Member/Secretary of this Council. The head office of Punjab Bait-ul-Mal is located at 31, M.P.C. Road (near Mashal Pahari), Lahore. Its telephone number is 042-99204157 and fax number is 042-36360023.

(3) For harmony and coordination in the equitable distribution of Bait-ul-Mal, especially in view of the convenience of beneficiaries, District Bait-ul-Mal Committees have been established at the district level throughout Punjab, which consist of local individuals of impeccable character, honesty, God-fearing, and good reputation.
