B. If any non-official member of the Council wishes to resign for any reason, he shall submit his resignation to the Government through the "Amin" and shall continue to function as a regular member of the Council until the resignation is approved.

23. Duties of the "Amin" in the absence of the "Amin"
If the office of the "Amin" is temporarily vacant, the Secretary Social Welfare, Women Development and Bait-ul-Mal shall perform the duties of the "Amin".

24. Duties and Powers of the Secretary Punjab Bait-ul-Mal Council
1- He shall be the in-charge of the administrative affairs of the Council and shall be responsible for running the administrative affairs of the Council in accordance with the rules and policies of the Council.

2- He shall ensure proper implementation of the policies of the Council and the decisions of the "Amin" Council.

3- He shall initiate the necessary proceedings to convene the meeting of the Punjab Bait-ul-Mal Council at the scheduled date and venue on the instructions of the "Amin".

4- He shall record the proceedings (Minutes) of each meeting and shall submit them to the Punjab Bait-ul-Mal for confirmation within ten days of the conclusion of the meeting.

5- Within seven days of the confirmation of the proceedings of the meeting, he shall send a copy of the proceedings to every member of the Council; copies shall also be sent to the District Bait-ul-Mal Committees if any decision pertains to them.

6- He shall communicate the information of decisions related to the District Committees made in the meetings of the Council to the official addresses of the respective Committees and to the Chairmen of the District Committees at their residential addresses via separate correspondence within seven days of the confirmation of the proceedings of the meeting.

7- He shall maintain the accounts of the Punjab Bait-ul-Mal Fund. He shall have the accounts audited every year and [unclear] budget.
