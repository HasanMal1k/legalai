17- Quorum of the Meeting of the Finance Committee

The Chairman of the Finance Committee shall convene the meeting of the Finance Committee upon the advice of the "Amin" and shall preside over it; however, in the absence of the Chairman, any member among the members present at the meeting shall be elected to preside over the meeting. The quorum of the meeting shall consist of three members, including the Secretary of the Council and the Finance Committee. The Amin Punjab Bait-ul-Mal may participate in the meeting of the Finance Committee. In the event of the completion of the quorum, the Amin Punjab Bait-ul-Mal shall be considered a member of the committee. If the Secretary of the Council is unable to participate in the meeting for any reason, the Deputy Secretary shall perform the duties of the Secretary, provided that three members are present.

18- Formation of the Electoral Committee

The Council shall nominate six members from among its non-official members to the Electoral Committee and shall nominate one of them as the Chairman of the Electoral Committee. The Secretary of the Council / Deputy Secretary General Social Welfare shall be the functionary Secretary of the Electoral Committee.

19- Duties and Powers of the Electoral Committee
It shall decide on matters relating to the following:
1- Nomination of members of the District Committees and related electoral matters.
2- Financial assistance to voluntary welfare organizations and related matters.
3- Financial assistance up to one hundred thousand rupees to deserving individuals and related matters (the Council shall be authorized to approve financial assistance exceeding one hundred thousand rupees).
It may directly bring forward cases of such registered non-official organizations which it deems appropriate, or any public welfare projects that are not affiliated with any non-official welfare organization, and may approve appropriate financial assistance after identification by the relevant District Bait-ul-Mal Committee, District Secretary Committee, or any member of the Council.
