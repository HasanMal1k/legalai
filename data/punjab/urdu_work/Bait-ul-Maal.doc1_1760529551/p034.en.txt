8- A copy of the verified proceedings of the meeting shall be sent to the Council, in addition to the concerned Committee, within seven days.

9- He shall be responsible for preparing the following reports as per the requirements of the details given before them and sending them to the Head Office on the scheduled date with the signatures of the Chairman.

(ii) The report of the employees receiving aid from the Bait-ul-Mal as per the pro-forma mentioned in Schedule IV by the 28th of February every year.
