35- Procedure for Issuance of Relief Checks

The District Committee shall issue checks for all relief amounts in accordance with the following procedure prescribed by [unclear]:

(i) The District Bait-ul-Mal Committee shall issue relief checks to the beneficiaries.
(ii) The beneficiary (applicant) shall appear personally before the authorized person (Chairman / Secretary) of the District Bait-ul-Mal Committee for the receipt of the relief check.
(iii) The Chairman / Secretary shall check the original identity card of the beneficiary.
(iv) The relief check shall be issued in the name of the beneficiary, and the identity card number of the beneficiary shall be entered on the check.
(v) The Chairman / Secretary shall obtain the signature/thumb impression of the beneficiary on the back of the check and shall affix his full signature as per the prescribed procedure for its verification.
(vi) An attested photocopy of the beneficiary's identity card shall be attached to the check.
The Chairman / Secretary may attest the identity card of the beneficiary.
(vii) The concerned bank branch shall obtain the signature/thumb impression of the beneficiary on the back of the check and shall also receive a photocopy of the identity card from them.
(ix) Payment shall be made to the beneficiary in exchange for the check after full compliance with all the above regulations.
