A Regional Council member may recommend financial assistance up to five lakh rupees for the NGOs of his constituency to the Executive Committee / Council.
5- While making a recommendation for financial assistance, or after the approval of financial assistance, it may also recommend imposing or removing any condition on the NGO.
6- At the effort of the Regional Council members, it is required to identify and present the received applications to the relevant District Committee / Council before the commencement of the next meeting of the relevant Committee / Council; the report of such recommendations shall be submitted to the relevant District Committee / Council in a timely manner.
7- Upon information from the relevant District Committee of its region, it may participate in its meeting based on its own judgment. At such an occasion, it may also conduct a visit at any other time, request and inspect the record of the District Committee at the site, and shall inform the Chairman of the Regional Council regarding the issuance of any important instructions, whether in writing or orally, at the site.
8- Upon the invitation of an NGO in its region, it may, upon instruction, visit any NGO in its region that has received financial assistance from Punjab Bait-ul-Mal, and may send its recommendations/impressions to the relevant District Committee / Council for necessary action.
9- All Council members shall strive to cooperate fully with the "Trustee" of Punjab Bait-ul-Mal to run the affairs of Punjab Bait-ul-Mal in a proper manner and to act sincerely upon its lawful instructions, and if any non-governmental member is unable to fulfill his responsibilities, he may resign with dignity from the Committee and opt to separate from the Council.
