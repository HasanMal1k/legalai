1.12 The Final Committee shall decide on the approval of financial assistance up to five hundred thousand rupees for NGOs. The Council shall be informed of this decision in its next meeting.

1.12.1 The Final Committee may recommend financial assistance exceeding five hundred thousand rupees for an NGO, and upon such recommendation, the Council shall make a final decision on its approval or disapproval in its next meeting.

1.12.2 Approved assistance to NGOs below five hundred thousand rupees shall be issued by the Head Office within one month after the issuance of the minutes of the Final Committee's decision.

1.12.3 Payment of amounts exceeding five hundred thousand rupees shall be issued by the Head Office within one month after final approval by the Council.

1.20 The Council shall be authorized to take back the remaining amount not spent by March 31 from the funds allocated to NGOs by the District Committees and distribute it among the NGOs selected by it.

1.21 The District Secretariat Committee shall visit the non-governmental companion organizations in its district receiving aid from Bait-ul-Mal and shall submit a performance report to the Council through the District Committee by June 30, which shall confirm the proper use of funds or recommend disciplinary action against NGOs violating the terms of payment.

1.22 Six months after the issuance of the payment check, the NGO shall invite the Bait-ul-Mal Committee and the Council's representative for a visit to discuss the following:
(i) Whether further board proceedings have been conducted regarding the details of the office building project;
(ii) Whether the NGO has purchased the required equipment from the assistance amount;
(iii) Whether a separate account of the income and expenditure of the assistance amount has been maintained or not.
