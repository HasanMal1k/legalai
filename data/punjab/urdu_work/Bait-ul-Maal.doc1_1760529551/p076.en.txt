6.5 Upon completion of the patient's treatment or exhaustion of the approved financial assistance, the patient shall submit the account of treatment expenses, including vouchers, receipts, etc., to the District Committee.

6.6 The limit for financial assistance for treatment is up to 50,000 rupees (fifty thousand rupees). A patient may obtain it only once, provided that the cost of treatment is fifty thousand rupees. In the event that the amount of assistance in the first instance is less than fifty thousand rupees, the patient may apply a second time; however, the total amount of the first and second instances must not exceed the limit of fifty thousand rupees.

6.7 In an emergency, the Chairman of the District Committee or any Vice Chairman (provided that a notification authorizing them to exercise this power has been issued) may issue a cheque for the assistance amount in the name of the Welfare Association for Patients / [unclear] Institution of the concerned hospital, which must be ratified in the next meeting of the District Committee. Furthermore, if the patient is unconscious and not capable of submitting an application, their parents, guardians, or relatives may submit the application, and if the Chairman/Secretary of the patient's [unclear] (via identity card or certificate) can write and verify the patient's information in the application.

6.8 The Council has allocated 3 percent out of 8 percent of the funds for the treatment of poor and disabled children, specifically for the plastic surgery of women victims of violence and for providing hearing aids to deaf and mute children, so that these children can continue their education by attending school.

6.8.1 The hospital administration, members of the Council, or District Bait-ul-Mal Committees may send cases of women victims of violence to the Council for financial assistance for plastic surgery.
