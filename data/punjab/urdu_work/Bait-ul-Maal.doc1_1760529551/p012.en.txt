12
Rules of Procedure of the Punjab Bait-ul-Mal Council

The Punjab Bait-ul-Mal Council, under the powers conferred upon it under Section 12 of the Punjab Bait-ul-Mal Act 1991, hereby makes the following rules:

1. Name and Commencement
(1) These rules shall be called the Punjab Bait-ul-Mal Council Rules of Procedure 2003.
(2) They shall come into force forthwith.

2. Definitions
In these rules, unless the context otherwise requires:
a- "Government" means the Government of Punjab.
b- "Council" means the Punjab Bait-ul-Mal Council.
c- "Trustee" means the Trustee of Punjab Bait-ul-Mal who shall be the Chairman of the Council.
d- "Staff" means the Punjab Bait-ul-Mal staff.
e- "Associate Member" means that non-official member whose appointment has been made under Rules 3 and 4(10) of the Bait-ul-Mal Council Rules.
f- "Meeting" means a meeting of the Punjab Bait-ul-Mal Council.
g- "Chairman" means the Chairman of the District Bait-ul-Mal Committee.
h- "District Committee" means the District Bait-ul-Mal Committee.
i- "Act" means the Punjab Bait-ul-Mal Act 1991.
j- "Fund" means the Punjab Bait-ul-Mal Fund.
