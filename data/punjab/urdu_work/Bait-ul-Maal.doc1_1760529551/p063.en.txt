4.3 Conditions for Obtaining Educational Scholarships

4.3.1 The District Bait-ul-Mal Committee shall ensure that students from the entire district benefit from scholarship benefits through fair distribution. However, after providing a scholarship to one child from each eligible family, the Committee may, in the event of the availability of funds, approve scholarships for other children of the same family.

4.3.2 Applications of students shall be submitted by their parents or guardians. However, orphaned students, female students whose parents are not guardians, and those who possess their own identity cards may submit applications themselves. Including students whose parents are government employees (up to Grade 15) who, in view of their weak financial condition, cannot educate their children, may apply for scholarships for one or more children from Bait-ul-Mal, provided that they have not obtained scholarship funds for such children from any other source.

4.3.3 There is no restriction on the selection of subjects for obtaining scholarships; however, students pursuing professional courses such as MBBS, BDS, MCS, or Information Technology, Arts, Engineering, Mining, ML, Pathology, and Para-medical (Dentist, Biology Technician, etc.) shall be given preference over students studying in Arts subjects.

4.3.4 Separate applications and scholarship approvals shall be granted for high-level educational courses in industrial training institutes, colleges, and universities; i.e., a separate application must be submitted for the level of FA, FSc, etc., and a separate application for BA, BSc, etc. Similarly, for applications submitted for professional courses such as MA, MSc, MCS, MBBS, MBNs, Engineering, and Law, scholarship approval shall be granted for the entire course (such as the five-year MBNs course) but separately on the basis of eligibility.
