(3) Appointment, Status, and Constitution of the Punjab Bait-ul-Mal Council
The Government of Punjab shall, by notification, constitute a provincial council consisting of the "Ameen" of Punjab Bait-ul-Mal along with the following fifteen government and non-government members. The post of "Ameen" shall be honorary and the status of the "Ameen" shall be equivalent to that of an officer of Grade 22 of the Government, and in addition to this, the "Ameen" shall receive all salary benefits and allowances which are available to an officer of Grade 22 of the Government.

The term of the "Ameen" and non-government members of the Council shall be for three years, provided that the "Ameen" and non-government members shall be eligible for nomination for a further three years. However, no person shall be able to become the "Ameen" or a non-government member for more than two terms. However, the Government shall have the authority to dismiss the "Ameen" and non-government members at any time on the basis of negligence or misconduct.

Government Members

a. Ameen Bait-ul-Mal Punjab — Chairman
b. Secretary Department of Social Welfare, Women Development and Bait-ul-Mal — Member
c. Secretary to the Government of Punjab, Finance Department — Member
d. Secretary to the Government of Punjab, Education Department — Member
e. Director General Social Welfare, Women Development and Bait-ul-Mal — Member Secretary

Non-Government Members

Ten non-government members, who shall be in the majority in the Council, shall be taken from among social workers whom the Government shall nominate with the advice of the "Ameen".
