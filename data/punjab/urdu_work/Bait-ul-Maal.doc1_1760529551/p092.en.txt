(3) The Council shall consist of 15 government and non-government members and the "Ameen" shall be its head.
(4) The Government shall appoint the "Ameen" and, upon the advice of the "Ameen", shall nominate the other members of the Council.
(5) Non-government members shall be honorary and in the majority, and they shall be persons who are dedicated to basic social work in the fields of social work, education, or health.
(6) Sub-clauses (1) and (7) relating to the nomination of the "Ameen" and government members shall be for a term of three years, and they shall be eligible for re-nomination for a further three years; provided that no person shall be able to serve as "Ameen" or member for more than two terms.
(7) The Government may remove any non-government member, including the "Ameen", on the grounds of negligence.
(8) The Government may increase or decrease the number of members of the Council.

3- Functions of the Council
The Council shall manage the administration of Punjab Bait-ul-Mal and shall perform all functions related to Bait-ul-Mal that the Government may delegate to it.

4- Punjab Bait-ul-Mal
The Government may establish a fund in the name of Punjab Bait-ul-Mal in which funds shall be collected.
(a) Aid from the Government, the Federal Government, local authorities, and international Islamic organizations.
