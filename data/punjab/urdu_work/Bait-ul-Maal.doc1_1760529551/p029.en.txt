7- Persons participating in the elections of the local government, national, and provincial assemblies shall not be eligible for membership of the district committees.
The term of membership of the committee members shall be three years from the date of issuance mentioned in the notification of nomination. With the approval of the Council, any Chairman, Vice Chairman, or member may be nominated for a further reasonable period. In the event of the dissolution of any committee, the notification of the three-year nomination shall be deemed null and void.

8- The final authority for the dissolution of the district Bait-ul-Mal committees shall vest in the Bait-ul-Mal Council.

Method of Nomination
a. In the district committee, one member from among the non-official members, other than the Chairman, shall be nominated as Vice Chairman.
b. The election committee of the Council shall have the authority to nominate the Chairman of the district committee on the recommendation of the "Amin" of Bait-ul-Mal, and its approval may also be obtained via telephone. Approval shall be given by the Punjab Bait-ul-Mal Council.
c. The "Amin" shall have the authority to nominate the members of the district committee and the Vice Chairman, and to issue formal notifications, through consultation with the concerned member of the Council and the Chairman of the district committee, either formally, in writing, or via telephone.
d. The notification shall be presented for the information of the Council in the next meeting.
e. The District Officer of Social Welfare or any other nominated officer of the district, including a government member of the committee, shall be the Secretary.
f. The Director General of the Department of Social Welfare, Women Development, and Bait-ul-Mal shall, on the advice of the "Amin," issue notifications in each district for district officers or nominated officers of Social Welfare as Secretary / DDO (Drawing and Disbursing Officer).

28- Duties and Powers of the Chairman of the District Committee
1- He shall oversee the entire district Bait-ul-Mal committee and ensure that all work is in accordance with the decisions of the committee.
