8- Shall prepare the performance report of Punjab Bait-ul-Mal by the Punjab Bait-ul-Mal Council by December 31st of each year, and shall submit it to the Council by February 28th to be sent to the Governor of Punjab by March 15th. And shall send the report approved by the Council to the Governor of Punjab without any amendment or change.

9- In each district, shall nominate the District Social Welfare Officer or, in the absence of the same, the second officer of the District Social Welfare Department as Secretary of the District Bait-ul-Mal Committee upon the advice of the "Amin," and shall issue the notification of the said person as Secretary / DDO (Drawing and Disbursing Officer) of the District Bait-ul-Mal Committee.

10- Upon the advice of the "Amin," shall issue the program for the phase-wise internal audit of the District Bait-ul-Mal Committees and the external audit upon the advice of the external auditors, and shall bind all Secretaries of the District Bait-ul-Mal Committees to ensure the availability of records, documents, and explanations in the office during the scheduled dates of the audit.

25- Duties and Powers of the Member of the Council

1- Shall regularly attend all meetings of Punjab Bait-ul-Mal. In case of absence from any meeting, shall provide information via telephone or in writing to the Secretary of the Council or the Deputy Administrator of Punjab Bait-ul-Mal.

2- Shall abide by all collective decisions of Punjab Bait-ul-Mal and shall perform duties with complete integrity.

3- Shall properly screen the applications received by the District Bait-ul-Mal Committees from the Head Office of Punjab Bait-ul-Mal or within his region in a serial manner, and shall provide a correct report/recommendation on each application.
