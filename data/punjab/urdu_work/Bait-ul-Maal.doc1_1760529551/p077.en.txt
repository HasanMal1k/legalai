6.8.3 All recommended cases shall be presented before the Executive Committee of the Punjab Bait-ul-Mal Council, which has the authority to approve or disapprove them.

6.8.4 Upon approval by the Executive Committee, the Council shall transfer the required funds.

6.8.5 After the completion of treatment, the District Committee shall obtain the expense vouchers and receipts from the Hospital Association for the Rehabilitation of Patients so that the file can be completed.

(b) Procedure for payment for deaf and mute children:

6.8.6 The District Committee shall send all cases regarding deaf and mute children to the Executive Committee of Punjab Bait-ul-Mal along with its recommendations, which will grant approval or disapproval of the cases.

6.8.7 Upon approval of the Executive Committee, the District Committee shall have the authority to spend the approved amount (up to a maximum of 20,000 rupees) for the purchase of hearing aids from its own budget. In the event of a shortage of funds under this clause, additional funds may be provided by the Council to the District Committee upon its request and submission of evidence.

Note: Application forms shall be provided by the District Bait-ul-Mal Committee to the Associations for the Rehabilitation of Patients established in the District/Tehsil hospitals and to the relevant NGOs working in the district.
