It is a matter of great pleasure that in the context of the government's development goals and numerous economic and social issues, welfare organizations are playing a full and active role in the completion of small-scale projects at the local level by becoming the hands and feet of the government. Recognizing the extreme need for administrative, financial, and every possible financial and technical assistance of these organizations, an arrangement has been made for Punjab Bait-ul-Mal to provide 15 percent of its budget as assistance for the priority projects of needy non-governmental welfare organizations. Needy organizations can submit their applications on the prescribed form to their respective District Committee Bait-ul-Mal. Punjab Bait-ul-Mal may re-allocate the total sanctioned budget according to the circumstances.

1- Conditions for Obtaining Assistance

1.1 Assistance shall only be provided to NGOs and national organizations working on a non-commercial basis.
1.2 Only those non-governmental organizations shall be eligible for assistance which are registered with Punjab Bait-ul-Mal and have demonstrated basic performance in our basic procedure for at least one year after registration.
1.3 It shall be the specific responsibility that the need for it is felt within the scope of all funds received.
1.4 It shall be necessary to present the audited Annual Audit Report of the income and expenditures of the previous year along with the application as conclusive evidence.
1.5 All sources (including donations, tithes, waqf, fee funds, and assistance from various donor organizations, etc.) shall be maintained in the accounts of income and expenditures as per the method prescribed by the Council, and the Committee/Council shall be presented with the application.
