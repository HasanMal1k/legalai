7- In any meeting held under the chairmanship of the Chairman, in the event of a tie in the votes of the Committee members, the opinion of the Chairman shall be deciding.

8- He shall sign all cheques issued by the District Bait-ul-Mal Committee along with the Secretary of the Committee and shall supervise the signing of other records prepared under its policy and rules on the instructions of the Punjab Bait-ul-Mal Council.

9- The Chairman shall send his recommendations to the Council, keeping in view the conduct and character of the Secretary of the Committee under the rules of the Council in connection with the issuance of the annual agenda for the Secretary of the Committee. The Chairman shall keep in view the points mentioned in Schedule VI while making recommendations.

10- Before proceeding on long leave, the Chairman shall obtain formal permission from the "Amin" Bait-ul-Mal so that the Chairman may, on behalf of the Committee, be granted timely financial and administrative powers by the "Amin" Bait-ul-Mal to maintain the proceedings of the Committee.

11- Upon receipt of a complaint of corruption or irregularity against the Chairman or Vice-Chairman of the District Committee, the "Amin" Punjab Bait-ul-Mal shall initiate proceedings by exercising the powers to give [under] Rule (14) (21).

29- In the absence of the Chairman

1- If the Chairman of the District Committee is absent from any meeting, the Vice-Chairman shall preside over the meeting in his absence.

2- In case of the long leave of the Chairman or expected delay in the nomination of the Chairman, the "Amin" shall be authorized to give the Vice-Chairman the power to sign cheques, and the information regarding the notification of this matter shall be given to the Secretary of the concerned District.
