Chapter Number 2
(1) Policy
Since the Punjab Bait-ul-Mal has limited funds, it is endeavored that these funds be spent for the assistance and rehabilitation of poor people on a first-come, first-served basis. In this regard, a heavy responsibility is imposed on the members of the District Bait-ul-Mal Commissions to ensure with complete satisfaction and scrutiny that the assistance provided from the Bait-ul-Mal is necessary. Any recommendation or decision will ultimately be accountable before Allah Almighty.
(2) Sources of Punjab Bait-ul-Mal Funds
The annual sources of income for the Punjab Bait-ul-Mal are as follows:
1. Aid from the Provincial Government, Federal Government, local bodies, and international Islamic organizations.
2. Voluntary aid including donations, contributions, and charities.
(3) It is the duty of the members of the Punjab Bait-ul-Mal Council and District Commissions to endeavor to make desired additions to the Punjab Bait-ul-Mal funds from local sources so that wealthy individuals may give donations, contributions, charities, etc., openly to this fund, and this amount may be used for the assistance and rehabilitation of the poor and the needy. This action is our moral and religious duty.
(4) Purpose of the Punjab Bait-ul-Mal Fund
i. Assistance and rehabilitation of the destitute, widows, orphans, the insolvent, and the needy.
ii. Provision of financial assistance for the destitute, orphans, talented, and deserving male and female students.
iii. Provision of financial assistance for the treatment of disabled and insolvent patients.
