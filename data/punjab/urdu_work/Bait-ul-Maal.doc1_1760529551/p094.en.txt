8- Staff of the Council
The Council may appoint such employees as it deems necessary for the completion of its affairs, on prescribed procedures and conditions.

9- Delegation of Powers
The Council may delegate all its powers or any power acquired under this Act to any of its members, unconditionally or conditionally.

10- Accounts and Audit
(1) The audit of the accounts of the Council shall be conducted by an auditor appointed by the Council for that purpose, who shall be a Chartered Accountant in accordance with the Chartered Accountants Ordinance, 1961.
(2) The Council shall provide the auditor with all account books, documents, explanations, and information required for the purposes of the audit.
