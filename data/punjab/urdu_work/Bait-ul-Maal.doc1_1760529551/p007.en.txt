2 (5) Financial assistance shall not be provided from the Bait-ul-Mal Fund for the construction of personal buildings, purchase of land or plots, education and treatment in foreign countries, monthly maintenance, travel expenses, or return expenses. Every individual who has already received assistance from any other institution for rehabilitation or necessity shall be ineligible for assistance from the Bait-ul-Mal Fund. However, a person may apply for only one of the following: one-time assistance under necessity, educational scholarships for children, medical treatment, and dowry fund and educational assistance within one year. The decision regarding the eligibility of the application received shall be made by the Commission in the first year, keeping in view the availability of funds and the number of applications received. A person may apply for one-time assistance again under necessity after one year of receiving one-time assistance.

(6) District Bait-ul-Mal Treasuries: Budget, Distribution, and Details
The funds provided by the Punjab Bait-ul-Mal [Council] every year are bound to be spent on the following purposes as per the prescribed rates. However, the Punjab Bait-ul-Mal Council shall have the authority to make changes in the rates according to the circumstances and the law.

1- Assistance to non-governmental welfare organizations: 15 percent of the total fund.
2- One-time assistance for helpless disabled individuals, destitute women, and unemployed poor individuals: 20 percent of the total fund.
3- Educational scholarships for deserving disabled and orphan students: 25 percent of the total fund.
4- Assistance for the marriage of daughters of poor parents: 25 percent of the total fund.
