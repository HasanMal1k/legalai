1.8.6 The concerned NGO shall be bound to comply with the principle of the matching grant (1/3rd of the administrative amount), and on this basis, aid shall be released to the NGO.

1.8.7 While making a recommendation for aid for any NGO, the Council member may, either at the time of approval or after the approval of aid, recommend the termination of any condition of aid on the basis of reasonable cause to the Executive Committee/Council. Otherwise, the application shall be bound to reach the Council through the concerned District Committee by its own course.

1.10 (a) The Council shall select members of the Council / District Liaison Councils, Welfare Organizations (NGOs), District Committees, or other advertised NGOs from the 90 percent funds available with it, for whom there is a need for funds for developmental projects. This advertisement shall be published by the Council in two newspapers by July 15th every year.

1.11 The Council may approve aid for an NGO to any extent. And while approving aid or after the approval of aid, the Council member may, under Rule 42, on the basis of reasonable cause, terminate any condition of aid based on the recommendation of the District Committee or his own discretion. The Council shall have the verification of the required documents of the application conducted by the Secretary of the concerned District Committee, and it shall obtain the scrutiny/recommendation report through the member of the Council of that area or, if it deems appropriate, through the services of the members of the District Screening Committee.
