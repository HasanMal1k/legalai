1.8.3. However, on the basis of an acceptable request under Rule (5) 25, the Administrative Committee/Council may recommend any condition or termination of assistance. After receipt from the Council Member, the District Committee shall forward the request to the Head Office of the Council. After approval by the Administrative Committee/Council, the payment check to the concerned NGO shall be issued by the District Committee. The District Bait-ul-Mal Committee shall avail the services of the relevant District Secretariat Committee, established under Rule (3) 28 for the screening of non-governmental welfare organizations, under Rule 36. The District Secretariat Committee shall consist of four members; one member shall be taken from each Tehsil from the District Committee. The Secretary of the District Committee shall serve as the Secretary of the District Secretariat Committee, and the Chairman of the District Committee shall nominate one from the nominated members of each Tehsil as the Chairman of the District Secretariat Committee.

1.8.4. In the event of a valid complaint against the Chairman of the Secretariat Committee, the Punjab Bait-ul-Mal shall have the authority to appoint any other member of the District Secretariat Committee as Chairman at its own discretion.

1.8.5. A Council Member may recommend financial assistance up to five lakh under Rule (4) 25 to active NGOs working in the districts included in his/her jurisdiction from 9 percent of the funds of his/her constituency, and may, upon the proposal of the District Coordination Council or on his/her own initiative by identifying an NGO, issue instructions to request payment from Bait-ul-Mal.
