14- Travel and Daily Allowance
The non-official members of the Council shall be provided with travel and daily allowances for the performance of official duties in accordance with the prescribed rates of travel and daily allowance for Grade 20 officers of the Government of Punjab. The Secretary of the Council shall approve the bills of these expenses.

15- Constitution of the Finance Committee
The Council shall nominate four members from among its non-official members to the Finance Committee and one of them as the Chairman of the Finance Committee. The Secretary of the Council / Director General Social Welfare shall be the ex-officio Secretary of the Finance Committee.

16- Functions and Powers of the Finance Committee
It shall make policies and decisions regarding the following matters:
1- To decide on matters relating to the recovery of estimated amounts of 5 to 10 lakh rupees from the district Bait-ul-Mal centers.
2- To submit proposals to the Council for the investment of Bait-ul-Mal funds.
3- To provide the ultimate method to encourage donors to collect maximum donations, charities, and alms in the Punjab Bait-ul-Mal Fund.
4- Matters relating to the assets and related affairs of the Bait-ul-Mal Fund.
5- To determine the powers of the Secretary Punjab Bait-ul-Mal, Chairman, and Secretary of the District Committee regarding expenditures on administrative matters from the Bait-ul-Mal Fund.
6- To determine the number of staff, their selection, determination of their salaries, recommendation of other benefits, and administrative matters related to staff from the Bait-ul-Mal Fund as per the requirements of the Council and District Committees, and to present them to the Council for approval.
