2.6.2 Those individuals who are receiving aid from the Pakistan Bait-ul-Mal, Zakat Fund, or any other institution for this purpose shall not be eligible for aid from the Punjab Bait-ul-Mal.

2.6.3 Aid shall not be provided for the purchase of land, plots, or the construction of personal houses.

Note: For emergency aid, preference shall be given to applicants, chronic patients, divorced and widowed women, minor daughters whose guardians are also elderly or incapacitated, elderly persons (aged 60 years or above), and persons with disabilities over other individuals.
