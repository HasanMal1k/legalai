To facilitate the needs of the Council and further its objectives, the Council may, upon the recommendation of the "Chairman," also nominate a maximum of two assistant members.

4- Functions and Powers of the Council

(1) The Council shall be authorized to establish District Bait-ul-Mal Committees to fulfill the objectives of Punjab Bait-ul-Mal and for the distribution of the Bait-ul-Mal Fund at the district level. These committees shall function under the general supervision and control of the Council, and the Council shall review their performance as required.

(2) The Council shall have the full authority to make rules under the Bait-ul-Mal Act 1991 and to make necessary regulatory amendments thereto.

(3) The Council shall formulate the policy regarding the income, distribution, and maintenance of accounts of the Punjab Bait-ul-Mal Fund and shall maintain general supervision and control over them.

(4) The Council shall be authorized to open an account for the Punjab Bait-ul-Mal Fund in any scheduled bank; checks for this joint account shall be issued with the signatures of the Chairman, Punjab Bait-ul-Mal, and the Secretary of the Council.

(5) The Council shall deposit an amount it deems appropriate every year in the Head Office of Punjab Bait-ul-Mal, and shall be authorized to spend an amount it deems appropriate every year in addition to the funds, and shall transfer the remaining amount to the District Bait-ul-Mal Committees as per the policy of the Council.

(6) The Council shall be authorized to order the District Committees to return the amount in their accounts to the Punjab Bait-ul-Mal Fund, and shall require the District Committees to transfer the mandatory amounts back to the Council's funds.

(7) The Council shall determine the banks for holding the transferred Bait-ul-Mal funds for the District Committees.

(8) The Council shall take appropriate measures for the investment of the Bait-ul-Mal Fund.
