33- Travel Expenses of Non-Official Members of District Committees
Non-official members of District Committees shall only be entitled to travel allowances in the course of disposal of applications as per the rate of travel expenses provided to officers of Grade 17 of the Government of Punjab, which shall not exceed 500 rupees per month. The authority to approve these expenses shall vest in the Chairman of the District Committee, provided that funds for this purpose are available in the administrative head and the approval of the Committee is obtained before payment.

34- Procedure for Proceedings of Meetings of the District Committee
1- The quorum of the meeting shall consist of at least one-half (51%) of the members, including the Chairman.
2- In the case of a meeting adjourned due to lack of quorum, the quorum for the subsequent meeting shall be one-third (1/3) of the total non-official members.
3- The decision of the Committee shall be that which accords with the majority opinion of the members present and voting in the meeting.
4- Every member shall also be able to express his views in writing.
