Due to non-Islamic customs and traditions, the desire for changes in human appearances, and ostentatious displays, the excessive expenses arising on social bonds and marriages have created a state of anxiety in society. Particularly, poor families often face severe financial difficulties to maintain their social status regarding their daughters. Consequently, to resolve the financial problems faced by poor families and to bear the burden of the marriage expenses of their daughters to some extent, a financial assistance scheme has been started. Under this scheme, financial assistance is provided by the District Bait-ul-Mal Committee for the marriage of a daughter.

5.1 Assistance for the marriage of poor daughters. Up to 35,000 rupees (per daughter). Daughters under the guardianship of any guardian, including granddaughters, nieces, and grand-nieces, fall within the meaning of "daughter."

Conditions for obtaining financial assistance for the marriage of daughters.

5.2 Needy parents or guardians whose daughters have reached marriageable age and have fixed a date for marriage should apply to the Bait-ul-Mal Committee, provided that they have not obtained assistance for this purpose from any other source.

5.3 Applicants may submit their application to the District Bait-ul-Mal Committee within 90 days of the date of fixing the marriage, along with the nomination of the marriage. After screening, financial assistance may be provided upon being declared eligible. While determining eligibility, the following priorities shall be kept in order:

i- Widows
ii- Poor guardians of orphaned daughters
iii- Poor and insolvent parents
