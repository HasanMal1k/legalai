Please inform the registry that, along with this advice, payment shall be made from the Bait-ul-Mal Fund in the event of their eligibility and availability of funds, and that they should avoid going repeatedly to the District Commissioner's office or the committee members and wait patiently for the notification of approval or disapproval of the application from the District Commissioner's office. A received return envelope should be used for this purpose. In the event of approval of the application, the District Commissioner shall strictly instruct the relevant beneficiaries through the return envelope to receive the payment check only on the designated date.

(b) After receiving the application, any member of the District Bait-ul-Mal Committee or their assistant shall visit the site to verify the identity, and shall examine the request of the applicant to reach the complete site of the project by prioritizing the applicant and recommending the verification of the qualifications, and then shall recommend the approval or disapproval of the payment to the District Committee in the context of all the circumstances of the case; following which, the District Committee, in light of the recommendation in its meeting, shall issue the payment check in the name of the applicant in the event of approval or notification.

(10) The District Bait-ul-Mal Committee shall ensure that the decision on every application is made within three months of the date of receipt, and the distribution of Bait-ul-Mal funds shall generally be on the basis of the "first come, first served" principle. However, serious or urgent nature applications shall be given priority. No specific amount shall be fixed for the members of this committee or for any tehsil of this district in the District Bait-ul-Mal funds, and overall, the District Bait-ul-Mal Committee shall be responsible for the distribution of funds.
