4.10 There shall be no restriction on the selection of subjects for the procurement of scholarships; however, preference shall be given to students pursuing professional education such as Medical, Engineering, Homeopathic, and MBA, as well as those pursuing other courses in ICS, MACS, or Information Technology, over students studying Arts subjects.

4.11 The "Administrator" shall have the authority to approve a scholarship for any student upon the recommendation of the District Committee in any special case, and while approving the scholarship, may impose any condition to suspend or terminate the scholarship.

Priorities

4.12 The responsibility for determining the eligibility of the applicant shall rest with the District Bait-ul-Mal Committee, and while determining eligibility, the following priority matters shall be specifically taken into account:
4.12.1 Assessment of the total number of members of the applicant's family, especially the number of children pursuing education, and the actual financial status.
4.12.2 The student is an orphan.
4.12.3 The student is disabled and capable of continuing education.

Budget for Scholarships

4.13 Educational scholarships shall be issued on an annual basis at the beginning of the year.
4.14 The District Bait-ul-Mal Committee shall review the priorities in various categories of scholarships while spending the budget allocated for educational scholarships.
4.15 The District Committee is required to spend at least 2 percent of the funds allocated for educational scholarships on scholarships for disabled students and 10 percent on scholarships for girls.
