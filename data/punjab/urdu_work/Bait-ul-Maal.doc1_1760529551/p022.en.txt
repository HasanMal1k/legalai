-15- On any type of application, the Trustee shall have the power to decide and spend from the provincial fund earmarked for "Amin" under the head of accounts for the needy, and information of these expenditures shall be given in the next meeting of the Council.

-16- The Secretary of Bait-ul-Mal shall have the power to make necessary administrative expenses.

-17- If the Council is dissolved, he may participate in the committee reconstituted by the Government; however, in the event that the quorum is not complete in any meeting of the committee, the proceedings of the committee shall be considered [unclear].

-18- On the request of any concerned Bait-ul-Mal committee, the Trustee shall have the power to grant approval to transfer the fund from one head to another on the basis of eligibility; information of this shall be given in the next meeting of the Council.

-19- The Trustee shall have the power to return an amount of up to 10 lakh rupees unused in any building of any committee to the Council's fund.

-20- To exercise those powers which have been intentionally delegated to any other "Amin."

-21- The Secretary shall have the power to decide on applications received in the office in a manner other than the usual; the Trustee shall have the power to issue complex instructions regarding the presentation of applications of the needy in the meeting of the Administrative Committee; furthermore, he shall be able to reject any application that is against the Bait-ul-Mal policy.

-22- During his temporary absence or leave, the "Amin" may delegate his powers, in part or in full, to the Secretary of the Punjab Bait-ul-Mal Council.

-23- On the basis of the decision of the Council or the Administrative Committee, in the case of a check prepared with a name, if six months have passed since the date of the check, or in the event of the check being damaged for any reason or the signatory passing away, he may order the issuance of a new check in the name of the spouse of the signatory, or any adult member of the family of the deceased.

-24- Resignation of the "Amin" or the Council
(a) If the "Amin" of Punjab Bait-ul-Mal wishes to resign from his post, he shall submit his resignation to the Government and shall continue to work in the post of "Amin" (until the resignation is accepted) in the event of resignation.
