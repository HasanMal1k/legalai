4.3.5 The sponsor shall present the certificate of regular admission of the student to the educational institution.
4.3.6 The District Committee shall be authorized to approve or disapprove all types of educational stipends, and only students studying in educational institutions of government educational arrangements and registered NGOs working on non-commercial bases shall be eligible for stipends. However, if a student is studying in a government-affiliated private educational institution due to the lack of facilities or residential facilities in government institutions or government-approved NGOs within their respective district, the 'Ameen' Punjab Bait-ul-Mal may, after approval, declare such students eligible for stipends, but such cases should be rare.
4.4 The District Committee shall have the authority, with the prior approval of 'Ameen' Punjab Bait-ul-Mal, to pay an amount equivalent to the prescribed rate for hostel as an allowance to such students who are pursuing education in an educational institution in another city where hostel facilities are not available and they are residing in [unclear] [unclear] etc., provided that the student is studying in that institution and the sponsor presents the certificate to the committee confirming that hostel facilities are not available in the institution.
4.5 Documents shall be submitted with the application, which shall indicate that the student obtained at least 50 percent marks in the previous examination; however, students obtaining industrial training shall be exempt from this condition, but they shall be given stipends on the basis of merit, i.e., preference shall be given to students obtaining higher marks. In this manner, students up to the 10th class shall be exempt from the 50 percent marks condition. However, the condition of 50 percent marks in the 8th class shall remain for obtaining a stipend in the 9th class.
