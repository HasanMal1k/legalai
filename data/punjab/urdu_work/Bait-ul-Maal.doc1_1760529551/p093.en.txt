5- Use of Bait-ul-Mal
(1) The Council shall manage the Bait-ul-Mal in a simplified procedure and use it for the following purposes:
i. Assistance and welfare of the poor and needy, especially poor widows and orphans
ii. Educational assistance for poor and deserving students
iii. Medical assistance for the poor
iv. For charitable purposes
v. Other purposes of public interest, especially where the beneficiaries belong to the underprivileged members of society.
vi. For other purposes approved by the Council.
(2) Under the general supervision of the Council, the distribution of the Bait-ul-Mal fund shall be through the district Bait-ul-Mal committees formed by the Council for these purposes, in accordance with the procedure prescribed by the Council.
6- Proceedings of the Council
The meetings and proceedings of the Council shall be conducted in accordance with the prescribed procedure and rules, and until such procedure is prescribed, in accordance with the method prescribed by the "Law".
