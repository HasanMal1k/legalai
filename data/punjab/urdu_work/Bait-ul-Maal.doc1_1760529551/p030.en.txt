2- It shall ensure that the Bait-ul-Mal Funds for the entire year, on the basis of the entire district, be distributed completely on a fair-share basis according to the "first come, first served" principle.

3- The Secretary Committee shall ensure that every application received by the committee is entered in the receipt register and that the applicant is issued a diary number according to the following sample receipt, and that the applicant is assured of the necessary action and information regarding the decision on the application. For this purpose, after an initial inquiry and verification through a district scrutiny committee, all received applications shall be categorized and divided into the following three categories. The district scrutiny committee shall consist of four members. The Secretary of the district committee shall also serve as the Secretary of this committee. One member shall be nominated from each relevant department, and the Chairman of the district scrutiny committee shall be nominated.

(i) Applications of a life-threatening nature shall be provided with first aid and primary treatment.

(ii) Eligible applicants shall be provided with immediate treatment and primary treatment in the case of urgent need.

(iii) Applicants who are clearly ineligible shall be informed immediately of their ineligibility so that they do not wait unnecessarily.
