(7) Any amount deposited into the Bait-ul-Mal funds in the form of donations, contributions, alms, and offerings from any source shall be deposited into its bank account. The Council shall provide a matching grant to the respective committee from the reserve funds for this amount, and the committee shall have the authority to spend the total amount collected by itself, the profit thereon, and the profit received by the Council on investments made from the amount returned to the Council as per the Council's instructions, on any items as per its own discretion. However, the district committee shall provide financial assistance from the funds received from the Council and its own collected funds only to such individuals or volunteers who are themselves pious and self-sufficient.
(8) Annual Budget
The Bait-ul-Mal Council Punjab prepares the annual budget of Bait-ul-Mal, and the financial resources available to the Council are allocated under a predetermined formula.
(a) 4 percent of the Bait-ul-Mal funds are earmarked for the following purposes. The Council may reallocate this as per requirements.
i. 2 percent for the administrative affairs of the Council.
ii. 2 percent for the "Amin" special fund for the assistance of the needy.
(b) The Council may retain some funds from its annual budget as it deems necessary, and the remaining funds shall be distributed among the district Bait-ul-Mal committees under the following principles:
20 percent to all district committees on an equal basis.
80 percent based on the population of the district of each district committee.
