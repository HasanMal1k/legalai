3 percent of the District Committee's budget is allocated for the administrative expenses of the Committee. The District Committee is authorized to spend these funds for the following purposes:

-1 Payment of salaries of the staff recruited by the District Committee as per the number of staff
-2 Payment of monthly telephone bills up to 1,500 rupees
-3 Payment of electricity and gas bills as per the cost of the bills
-4 Expenses for repair and refitting of iron cupboards as per requirement
-5 Expenses on NOVEL stores (after approval of Aman Punjab Bait-ul-Mal) as per requirement
-6 Miscellaneous expenses (photocopying, purchase of paper, bicycle repair, etc.) as per requirement

In addition, the District Committee shall keep 2,000/- rupees as a permanent Imprest Money with the Secretary of the District Committee for small daily expenses for which a cheque cannot be issued, and may spend it through this with the approval of the Chairman of the Committee.
