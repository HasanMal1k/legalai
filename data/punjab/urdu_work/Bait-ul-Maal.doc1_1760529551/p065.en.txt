4.5.1 Orphan students shall attach a death certificate of their parents along with their application.
4.5.2 Disabled students shall attach a disability certificate issued by the District Review Board / Medical Superintendent of the District / Tehsil Hospital so that the District Bait-ul-Mal Committee can give the student's disability the status of acceptable evidence.
4.5.3 A certificate of the total monthly income of the parents / guardian shall have to be presented, which proves that they are not capable of continuing the child's education. In the event that the parents / guardian are employees, this certificate shall be issued by the Employer. Otherwise, the relevant member of the District Committee recommending the application for aid shall provide the details of the monthly income of the parents / guardian.
4.6 Educational scholarships shall be issued on an annual basis according to the duration of the course; however, in the event that the duration of the course exceeds one year, the installments for the following year shall be issued when the student, along with the marks obtained in the annual examination passed in the first attempt, provides the college / university's transcript certificate and details every year at the time of taking the installment, stating that they are continuing their education.
4.6.1 Payment of scholarships shall be made in the form of a cheque in the name of the parents / guardian of the student as per the procedure prescribed by the [unclear], and the information regarding the issuance of the cheque to the parents / guardian shall also be provided to the concerned student.
4.7 In the opinion of the District Bait-ul-Mal Committee, if the parents / guardian of any student are capable of paying a portion of their children's educational expenses, then in such a case, the Committee shall be authorized to reduce the amount of the monthly scholarship to the maximum extent.
4.8 The District Bait-ul-Mal Committee may withdraw the scholarship facility or the amount of the scholarship paid in case of fraud or without any reason from any student.
4.9 Educational scholarships shall not be issued from the Bait-ul-Mal funds for obtaining education abroad.
