10- Adjournment of Meeting
No meeting shall be adjourned temporarily except that:
1- The quorum is complete.
2- For any other reason which the "Trustee" records.

11- Absence of "Trustee" or Members from Meeting
1- If the "Trustee" or any non-official member is unable to attend the meeting, it shall be necessary for them to provide written notice to the Secretary Punjab Bait-ul-Mal Council or Deputy Secretary (Bait-ul-Mal).
2- Due to official engagements, the official members of the Council, namely the Secretary Social Welfare, Secretary Department of Finance, and Secretary Department of Education, shall only be represented by officers up to the rank of Deputy Secretary.

12- Removal of Council Member
1- If any member of the Council is absent from three consecutive meetings without notice or without any valid reason, the "Trustee" may recommend to the Bait-ul-Mal Government the termination of that member's membership.
2- If any member is found to have committed any misconduct or irregularity, the Government shall have the authority to remove them upon the recommendation of the "Trustee".

13- Minutes of Council Meeting
1- The minutes of every meeting shall be recorded in the English language.
2- In the next meeting, the minutes of the previous meeting shall be confirmed, after such amendments as may be proposed and approved.
