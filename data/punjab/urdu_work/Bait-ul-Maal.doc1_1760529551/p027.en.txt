8- Shall arrange for the annual audit of the accounts and income and expenditure of the Council. And with the cooperation of the Office of the State Director, shall provide the required documents, explanations, and replies to the auditors and shall prepare the annual budget of the Head Office.

9- The "Amin" Secretary of the Council, under the instructions of the [unclear], shall issue meeting notices with agenda and working papers to the members of the Council seven days prior to the requirements of each meeting, so that every member receives the notice seven days before the meeting. One day before the meeting, he shall remind every member of participation in the meeting through telephone or other means.

10- Shall assist the Secretary of the Council in recording the minutes of each meeting and, on the day of conclusion of each meeting, shall send a copy of the minutes to the members of the Council for information and necessary action within seven days, after obtaining confirmation from the "Amin" from the internal office of the Secretary of the Council.

11- Shall be bound to implement the decisions made in the meetings of the Council and the Financial and Administrative Committees in accordance with the instructions of the "Amin" Secretary.

12- Shall be personally responsible for the preparation of answers to questions from the Provincial Assembly and, after approval, for the timely delivery of the answers by the "Amin" Secretary of the Punjab Bait-ul-Mal Council to the Administrative Department.

13- Other duties as may be assigned by the "Amin" Secretary.

14- At the time of his transfer, retirement, etc., shall be responsible for preparing a list of the records, equipment, etc., of the Head Office and handing it over in writing to the incoming officer.
