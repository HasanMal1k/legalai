Those individuals who are victims of the calamities of the times and are suffering from any disease and do not have the means to bear the total or additional expenses of treatment, such persons and their relatives shall be provided financial assistance from the Bait-ul-Mal Fund. Needy patients may obtain the amount by referring to the relevant Bait-ul-Mal Committee and submitting an application on the attached prescribed form.

Along with the application form, the estimate of the proposed treatment expenses from the relevant authorities of the treatment center or the doctor must be attached. The relevant Committee shall decide on the eligibility of the patient, keeping in view the instructions of the relevant Bait-ul-Mal Fund and the estimate. The Chairman of the relevant Committee shall ensure that the funds are distributed fairly across the entire district on a first-come, first-served basis within six months of the year.

Conditions for financial assistance for treatment

6.1 The expenses of treatment must be beyond the reach of the patient.

6.2 The patient must wish to undergo treatment in local government hospitals or in the hospitals of any registered voluntary welfare organization.

Conditions for assistance for treatment abroad

6.3 Treatment must not have been provided through any other source or organization.

6.4 Financial assistance for treatment shall be provided through the association, patient welfare society, or the hospital of the relevant welfare organization established in the relevant hospital, instead of directly to the patient. However, in the event that treatment facilities for a patient are not available in the district/tehsil hospital, the district Committee shall have the authority to have the patient treated in another district. In such a case, the Committee of the patient's respective district shall issue a check for the payment of expenses to the Bait-ul-Mal Committee of that district where the patient is undergoing treatment; following this, this Committee shall transfer these funds to the association or patient welfare society of the relevant hospital.
