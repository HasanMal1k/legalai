16- He shall arrange for the annual audit of the Committee from the Chartered Accountant appointed by the Council, and shall provide all required documents and information to the Chartered Accountant for that purpose; he shall be personally present at the time of the Internal/External audit, and shall ensure the timely completion of the audit by cooperating with the auditors and the Chartered Accountant.

17- Upon receipt of the audit report, he shall take all necessary steps as soon as possible to remove the objections mentioned in the report in consultation with the Chairman.

18- He shall be personally responsible for keeping all records of the District Committee in the office of the Committee, and upon being relieved of his responsibilities due to resignation, retirement, suspension, or any other reason, he shall formally hand over all matters of the District Committee to the incoming or nominated officer of the District Committee, and shall submit a copy of the receipt to the Chairman of the District Committee.

19- He shall be personally responsible for the preparation of answers to the questions of the Provincial Assembly regarding the District Bait-ul-Mal Committee, and for sending them to the Head Office of the Punjab Bait-ul-Mal Council, District Committee Chairman, in a timely manner after approval.

31- Duties and Powers of the Member of the District Committee

1- He shall regularly attend all meetings of the District Bait-ul-Mal Committee. In case of absence from a meeting, he shall provide written information to the Chairman or Secretary of the Committee.

2- He shall abide by all decisions of the District Bait-ul-Mal Committee and shall perform his duties with complete honesty.
