20- Quorum of the Electoral Committee Meeting

The Chairman of the Electoral Committee shall convene the meeting of the Electoral Committee on the advice of the "Amin" and shall preside over it. In the absence of the Chairman, the members present at the meeting shall elect any member among themselves to preside over the meeting. The quorum of the meeting shall consist of five members including the Secretary Council. If the Secretary Council is unable to attend the meeting for any reason, the meeting may be convened on the request of the Deputy Secretary Council so that five members are present. The "Amin" may participate in the meeting of the Punjab Bait-ul-Mal Electoral Committee. In the event of a lack of quorum, the "Amin" shall be considered as the worker of Punjab Bait-ul-Mal.

21- Powers and Duties of the "Amin"

1- The "Amin" shall manage the Punjab Bait-ul-Mal Council and shall be responsible for running the affairs of the Bait-ul-Mal as a "Qain-e-Hasan" (exemplary model) under the Punjab Bait-ul-Mal Act, the rules and policies of the Council, especially including the public monitoring and control of the public Bait-ul-Mal Committee.

2- The "Amin" shall obtain assurance from the Secretary Punjab Bait-ul-Mal Council that all policies and decisions of the Council are being implemented correctly.

3- The "Amin" may, at his own discretion, call for a meeting of the Punjab Bait-ul-Mal Council at appropriate times at such a place as is necessary for deciding the matters of Punjab Bait-ul-Mal.

4- He may postpone the meeting at his own discretion for any other reason, but in any case, the reason for such postponement must be communicated.

5- In any such meeting held under the chairmanship of the "Amin," if there is a vote on any issue, in the event of a tie in the votes of the members, the opinion of the "Amin" shall be decisive.

6- He shall be authorized to grant the right to participate in the discussion of the Council's meeting to any insightful and distinguished personality.

7- He shall have the power to approve the leave of the Chairman of any relevant committee.
