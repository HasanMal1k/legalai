Punjab Bait-ul-Mal Act 1991
(Act No. 7 of 1991)
Including Amendments 1994
(First published in the Extraordinary Gazette No. 30 March 1991 after the Attestation of the Governor of Punjab)
Act for the Establishment of a Charitable Fund named Punjab Bait-ul-Mal

Preamble: Whereas it is deemed expedient that a charitable fund be established in the name of Punjab Bait-ul-Mal, therefore the following law is enacted.

1. Short Title, Extent and Commencement
(1) This Act shall be called the Punjab Bait-ul-Mal Act 1991.
(2) It shall extend to the entire province.
(3) It shall come into force immediately.

2. Bait-ul-Mal Council
(1) The Government may, by way of notifications, establish a council in the name of Bait-ul-Mal Council and may at any time complete it.
(2) The Council shall be a body corporate having the status of a legal entity, having the power to acquire and hold property
