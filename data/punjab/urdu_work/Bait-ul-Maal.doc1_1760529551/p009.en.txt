(9) Applications for Assistance

(a) The head office of Punjab Bait-ul-Mal is located at Nazamat Samai-e-Behbood, 41-Incharian Road, Lahore. For the distribution system of Bait-ul-Mal funds, particularly for the convenience of the beneficiaries, a District Bait-ul-Mal Committee has been established in every district of Punjab, which is authorized to distribute the relief amount in its respective district in accordance with the rules.

Application forms etc. can be obtained in person or through a representative, either by hand or by post. In the case of obtaining the form by post, it is necessary to send a reply envelope along with the letter, on which the applicant's address must be درج (entered). It is necessary that the application form be stamped by the District Committee.

In the case of sending the application form and supporting documents to the District Bait-ul-Mal Committee office by post, two reply envelopes should be attached; in the case of submission in person or through a representative, one envelope should be attached to the application form. The committee members shall ensure that all received applications are forwarded to the District Committee as soon as possible.

For applications received by post, a diary number shall be assigned and all instructions shall be issued to the applicant by the committee via post; whereas, in the case of submission in person to the committee, the diary number shall be provided on the spot and the applicant shall be informed of the position of their application in the queue of previously received applications, and the approximate time required for this process shall be communicated. Similarly, instructions for applications received by post or from committee members shall also be sent to the applicants so that they may be spared the inconvenience of repeatedly visiting the office.

If possible, duty rosters shall be prepared so that a member of the District Committee office can be available at different times, so that no applicant faces delays in submitting the application and obtaining complete information.

The District Committee is required to immediately allot a diary number to all applications received by post, in person by the applicant, or through committee members, and communicate the same to the respective applicant by post or on the spot.
