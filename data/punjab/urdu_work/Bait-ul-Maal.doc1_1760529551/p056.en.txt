2.3 If any application cannot be settled promptly on the basis of reasonable grounds, the District Committee shall have the authority to wash out (cancel) the received application after six months at its own discretion, so that the affected applicant may submit a fresh application to the Committee in view of their extreme necessity.

2.4 There shall be no criteria for the distribution of funds at the level of committee members or the Tehsil level for expenses, and decisions regarding the approval of financial assistance based on the entire district shall be made by considering the temporary nature of the funds and only the number of applications.

2.5 The maximum limit for one-time assistance is two thousand rupees. An applicant can receive assistance once in a year. It is mandatory for the District Committee to ensure that, while distributing assistance under this head, the funds are spent in a transparent and fair manner across the entire district for the whole year by following the eligibility criteria, so that only eligible applicants may benefit from it.

2.5.1 Needy women are requested to approach the Bait-ul-Mal Committee of their district to obtain one-time assistance for their temporary needs and submit an application on the prescribed/specified form.

2.5.2 The order of priority for applications received for assistance shall be as follows:
(1) Persons with disabilities. (2) New individuals. (3) Widows, helpless, and elderly poor individuals. (4) Marriage of daughters. (5) Educational scholarships.
