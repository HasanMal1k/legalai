12. Power to Make Rules
The Council may make rules for the achievement of the objectives of this Act.

13. Dissolution of the Council
The Government may dissolve the Council by way of notification in the official Gazette and may issue such relevant and consequential orders as may be necessary for that purpose.

14. Repeal
The Punjab Bait-ul-Mal (Amendment) Ordinance, 1994 (V of 1994) is hereby repealed.
