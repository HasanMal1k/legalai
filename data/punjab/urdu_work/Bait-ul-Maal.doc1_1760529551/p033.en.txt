30- Duties of the Secretary of the District Bait-ul-Mal Committee

1- He shall be the in-charge of the fund of the District Bait-al-Mal Committee at the district level.

2- He must be aware of the fact that as Secretary of the District Committee, he is bound to provide every possible exemption, guidance, and cooperation to the Chairman, and in this regard, he shall work with full honesty, responsibility, and teamwork to perform the Committee's work in the best manner, and shall ensure the best performance of the Committee in accordance with the instructions, policies, and rules of the Council, and shall not take any such step which may give the impression that he is trying to run the Committee according to his own will (Dictating to the Committee). Rather, he shall ensure that no decision of the Committee is against the rules of the Council and no rule is violated at the time of issuing any payment check.

3- He shall enter all received amounts in the diary register and allot diary numbers, and the Chairman of the Committee shall be bound to promptly process them as soon as possible and implement the instructions/decisions upon them. Similarly, he shall arrange for the issuance of challans and payment checks based on the "first-come, first-served" basis by entering received applications in the diary register and according to the diary numbers.

4- He shall enter applications sent by the members of the District Committee for the issuance of challans in a separate register and shall record the date of dispatch and the proposed date of return in it. In case of delay from the part of the concerned members, he shall bring this matter to the notice of the Chairman of the Committee.
