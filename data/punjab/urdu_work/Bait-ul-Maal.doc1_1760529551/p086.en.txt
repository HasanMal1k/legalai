Schedule VI: Performance Evaluation for Granting Honorarium to Secretaries of District Bait-ul-Mal Committees

Before the approval of the recommendation for the honorarium of the Secretaries of the District Bait-ul-Mal Committees, the performance of the Chairman of the District Bait-ul-Mal Committee and the "Main" Punjab Bait-ul-Mal Secretary shall be reviewed, and on satisfactory performance, they shall be declared eligible for an honorarium of Rs. 500/- per month in whole (In Whole). Otherwise, the Secretary shall be eligible for an honorarium in part (In Part) as per the Secretary's performance, i.e., less than Rs. 500/- (as approved by the "Main" District Committee / "Main" Punjab Bait-ul-Mal).

The performance of the Secretaries shall be evaluated based on the following points at the time of submission:

-1 Is the mail received from the Punjab Bait-ul-Mal Head Office shown to the Chairman without delay, and is it then read out to the members for information in the next meeting of the District Committee?
-2 Are the meetings of the District Bait-ul-Mal Committee called regularly on the scheduled dates?
-3 Are the members of the Committee sent the agenda on time? Except for the fifth meeting, the agenda should be sent to the members 7 days before the meeting.
-4 Is the Committee guided to a certain extent in making decisions according to the policy/rules of the Constitution?
-5 How many times were objections/explanations received from the Head Office regarding the proceedings of the District Committee's meeting?
-6 To what extent are the implementation of the meeting's decisions and the proceedings of the meeting addressed regarding objections/explanations/impressions?
-7 Are the proceedings of the District Bait-ul-Mal Committee's meeting submitted to the Chairman for approval/confirmation on time?
-8 Is a copy of the meeting proceedings sent to all members of the Committee and the "Main" Punjab Bait-ul-Mal within 7 days of approval by the Chairman?
-9 Is the monthly report of expenditures and the bank statement sent to the "Main" Punjab Bait-ul-Mal on time?
