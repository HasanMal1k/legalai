Foreword
After the establishment of Punjab Bait-ul-Mal in 1991, a booklet was published in the name of Punjab Bait-ul-Mal to increase information and provide guidance to the public, especially the deserving and needy. However, with the passage of time, due to necessary changes and amendments in the laws, policies, and rules regarding the distribution of the Bait-ul-Mal Fund, it was felt that this booklet needed to be revised. In this regard, after revision, the latest information has been presented in a comprehensive manner with the hope that readers can obtain guidance from it and fully benefit from the Bait-ul-Mal Fund. This fund is a trust of society for the deprived and destitute individuals, and delivering it to the deserving and needy with sincere intentions is the motto of Punjab Bait-ul-Mal and an important part of this policy. We are grateful for the practical assistance and useful opinions of those who keep simplicity and honesty in mind, especially the wealthy men and women, in this regard. Because such actionable suggestions will help in making Bait-ul-Mal a more reliable and performance-oriented institution.

Under Section 12 of the Punjab Bait-ul-Mal Act 1991 (amended 1994), the Punjab Bait-ul-Mal Council has been given the powers to make rules. Using these powers, the Council has formulated the policy and rules in light of the aforementioned Act. The District Bait-ul-Mal Units shall present the policy, rules, and procedures of Punjab Bait-ul-Mal for the distribution of the Bait-ul-Mal Fund as written in this booklet for their guidance. All District Units are requested to follow these rules and instructions with heart and soul for the completion of their official affairs.

Thanks to the public relations, general administration, and social welfare departments for the compilation and editing of this booklet.

"Amin"
Punjab Bait-ul-Mal Council, Lahore
