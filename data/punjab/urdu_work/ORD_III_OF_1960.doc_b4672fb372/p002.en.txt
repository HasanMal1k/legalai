"8-a- The Government of West Pakistan shall be authorized to delegate the powers of a Magistrate obtained under this Code to any person in respect of a particular case, or cases of a particular type or types, or generally in respect of cases of a particular area, to such person as Special Magistrate—

3- The existing Section 9 of the Criminal Procedure Code of the State of Qalat, 1951, shall be deemed to be sub-section (1) of the said section, and the following sub-section (2) shall be added:

"(2) The Government of West Pakistan shall have the authority to delegate the powers of investigation and prosecution of the cases mentioned in sub-section (1) to any Special Magistrate—the courts of such Special Magistrate whether within the districts of Qalat, Kharan, Lasbela, and Makran or in any part of West Pakistan outside the aforementioned districts,"

In Section 9 of the Criminal Procedure Code of the State of Qalat, 1951, after the words "may give orders," the words "whether such place be within the districts of Qalat, Kharan, Lasbela, and Makran or in any part of West Pakistan outside the aforementioned districts," shall be added.
