And since the Provincial Assembly of Punjab has not met and the Governor of Punjab is informed that the circumstances demand that immediate action be taken.

Therefore, I, the Governor of Punjab, in exercise of the powers conferred upon me under Article 128, sub-section (1) of the Constitution of the Islamic Republic of Pakistan, do hereby make and promulgate the following Ordinance:-

1- Short Title and Commencement:- (1) This Ordinance shall be called the [unclear] Ordinance (Amendment) Punjab Ordinance 2015.
(2) It shall come into force immediately.

2- Amendment in Section 13 of Ordinance 20 of 1965:- In Ordinance Pakistan Ordinance 1965 (20 of 1965), for Section 13, the following expression shall be substituted:

"13- Offence and Punishment:- (1) If any person commits any of the following offences, he shall be liable to punishment under sub-section (2):

(a) Selling, possessing, offering for sale, or offering to any person, any weapon, ammunition, or explosives in violation of Section 4;
(b) If the notice of sale of weapons and ammunition required under Section 4 and the name and address of the person giving it are not provided;
(c) Transporting any weapons, ammunition, or explosives in violation of the regulations or trust entrusted under Section 5;
(d) Going out armed in violation of Section 6;
(e) Keeping any weapons, ammunition, or explosives in one's possession or control as per Section 9;
(f) Failing to deposit weapons, ammunition, or explosives as per the provisions of Section 10;
(g) Clearly entering in the record or register of the person required to maintain it under the rules made under sub-section (d) of Section 11:"
