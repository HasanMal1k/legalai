-3-

(i) Any person who, in contravention of the rule prescribed under the proviso to Section 11, fails to display any item required to be displayed as per the rule; or
(ii) Any person who, in violation of the order issued under Section 11, keeps any weapon in his possession, or carries it with the intent to dispose of it.

(2) If any person commits any offense mentioned in sub-section (1) and the offense is of such nature as to involve:
(a) a non-prohibited bore weapon, weapons of non-prohibited bore, or a firearm of a prohibited nature, he shall be liable to imprisonment for a term of two to seven years along with forfeiture of the seized property;
(b) a prohibited bore weapon or weapons of prohibited bore, he shall be liable to imprisonment for a term of four to seven years along with forfeiture of the seized property;
(c) when a person possesses two or more prohibited bore weapons, or more than five non-prohibited bore weapons, or one prohibited bore weapon and two non-prohibited bore weapons, he shall be liable to imprisonment for a term of five to fifteen years along with forfeiture of the seized property.

(3) The Government shall classify non-prohibited and prohibited bore weapons by way of notifications or rules for the purposes of Section 11.
