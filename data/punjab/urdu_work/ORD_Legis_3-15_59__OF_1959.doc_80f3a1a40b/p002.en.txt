Section No. 2 — Enforcement of the Code of Criminal Procedure, 1954 by virtue of the Resolution of the Governing Council of the State of Balochistan, dated April 22, 1952, notwithstanding whether such enforcement is in accordance with the Provisional Constitution of the State of Balochistan or not — in any case, shall be deemed correct, and all proceedings, imposed duties, responsibilities, fines, appointments or transfers of duties, divestment of powers, jurisdiction of the court, orders, etc., under this Code shall be deemed to be valid.

Amendment of the Code of Criminal Procedure, 1954
Section No. (1) — In the Code of Criminal Procedure, 1954, wherever the words "District Magistrate" are written, they shall mean Magistrate (Sub-Divisional Magistrate Grade I) — except in those provisions where specifically any other word has been written in place of "District Magistrate" by virtue of the amendments mentioned in sub-section (2).

(2) In the Code of Criminal Procedure, 1954, the following amendments shall be made:—
