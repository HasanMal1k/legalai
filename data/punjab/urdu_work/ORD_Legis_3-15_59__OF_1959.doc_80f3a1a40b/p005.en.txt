Gazette of West Pakistan (Extraordinary) March 30, 1959
235

Number | Chapter | Sections for Amendment | Amendments

Fifth Chapter | Section No. 22 | The entire phrase after the words "may be issued" in sub-section (4) shall be deleted.
| Section No. 23 | The words "Prime Minister" shall be replaced by the words "Government of West Pakistan".
Seventh Chapter | Section No. 3 | Between the words "kendah" and "ko", the words "ya adalat daryaqt kendah" shall be inserted.
Eighth Chapter | Section No. 9 | In the first paragraph, the words "Prime Minister Sahib Bahadur" shall be replaced by the words "Provincial Government" and read accordingly, and the following condition shall be added after this paragraph:
"The condition is that such any order shall not be against any previous order of the High Court of West Pakistan."
Ninth Chapter | Section No. 58 | In sub-section (4), the word "Nazim" shall be replaced by the words "Magistrate hearing" and read accordingly.
Tenth Chapter | Section No. 60 | In sub-section (1), the words "before the Prime Minister Sahib Bahadur" shall be replaced by the words "before the District Magistrate" and read accordingly.
| Section No. 62 | The phrase "under special circumstances with the permission of Prime Minister Sahib Bahadur" shall be deleted.
Eleventh Chapter | Section No. 65 | In sub-section (1), the words "Prime Minister" shall be replaced by the word "Court" and read accordingly.
