Amendments to the Provisions of Chapter XII
Section Number 67
1. In sub-section (1), the words "Government of Qalat" shall be substituted by the words "Government of West Pakistan" and the words "Prime Minister" shall be substituted by the words "Commissioner Qalat Division".
2. In sub-section (3), the words "Minister of Justice or Home Secretary" and the words "Prime Minister" shall be substituted by the words "District Magistrate".

Section Number 68
1. In sub-section (1), the words "Prime Minister Sahib" shall be substituted by the words "District Magistrate".
2. The entire contents of sub-section (2) shall be deleted and the following text shall be substituted in its place:
"The approval for the appointment of the members of the Provincial Journal shall be obtained from the District Magistrate."
In sub-section (2), the words "Ministry of Justice or Home Secretary" shall be substituted by the words "District Magistrate".

Section Number 69
The words "Prime Minister" shall be substituted by the words "District Magistrate".

Section Number 70
The words "Prime Minister" shall be substituted by the words "District Magistrate".
