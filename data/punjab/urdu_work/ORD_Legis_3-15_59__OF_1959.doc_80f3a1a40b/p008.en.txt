Gazette of West Pakistan (Extraordinary) March 30, 1959
Number Chapter Provisions Amendable
Amendments

Sixteenth Chapter
Section Number 111: Be read and written as follows: In sub-section (b), the words "Judicial Commissioner or Home Secretary" be substituted by the words "District Magistrate" and be read and written as such. And the word "Province" after the word "Administrator" be deleted.

Section Number 113: In sub-section (4), the words "Judicial Department" be substituted by the words "District Magistrate concerned" and the words "State Capital" be substituted by the words "other districts" and be read and written as such.

Eighteenth Chapter
Section Number 116: In sub-section (4), the words "Prime Minister" be substituted by the words "District Magistrate" and be read and written as such.

Section Number 119: The words "Prime Minister by order" be substituted by the words "Government of West Pakistan shall enforce" and be read and written as such.

Section Number 123: The words "Prime Minister - Sessions Judge or District Magistrate" be substituted by the words "High Court of West Pakistan - Sessions Judge or Magistrate of the First Class" and be read and written as such.

Nineteenth Chapter
Section Number 127: The words "District Magistrate" be substituted by the words "Administrator and District Magistrate" and be read and written as such.

Section Number 128: In place of Section 128, the following sections 128 and 128 (a) shall be substituted:—
"128 - Appeal against the recommendation of the District Magistrate.
An appeal against the recommendation of the District Magistrate shall be heard in the Court of Sessions."
