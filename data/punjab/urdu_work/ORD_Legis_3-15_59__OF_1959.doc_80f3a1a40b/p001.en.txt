NOTIFICATION
Dated: 3 March 1959
Number: 59(15)3 Leg. - The following Ordinance is hereby published for the information of the public by order of the Governor of West Pakistan -

Code of Criminal Procedure (Amendment West Pakistan) Ordinance, 1959
West Pakistan Ordinance No. [unclear] of 1959

ORDINANCE
For the amendment of the Code of Criminal Procedure, 1951

WHEREAS it is expedient that the Code of Criminal Procedure, 1951, be amended in the following manner -

WHEREAS the President's order dated 2 October 1958, and in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by the instructions of the President, the Governor of West Pakistan is to bring into force the following Ordinance -

Section 1 - This Act shall be called the Code of Criminal Procedure (Amendment West Pakistan) Ordinance, 1959.
