Chapter II -
Section 8 - Repealed
And Kharian shall be considered a Tehsil Division.
The power to make changes and alterations in the Tehsil Division shall vest with the Provincial Government of West Pakistan.
(2) In every district, the Provincial Government shall appoint a Magistrate of the First Class as the District Magistrate, who shall be called the District Magistrate.
(3) According to this code, every part of a district shall be considered a Tehsil and its Nazim shall have the powers of a Magistrate of the First Class.
Explanation - (1) The power to make changes and alterations in the boundaries of the districts shall vest with the Government of West Pakistan, which shall also be able to create new districts.
(4) Tehsil shall include Tehsils and Sub-Tehsils.
