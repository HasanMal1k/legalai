Whereas it is a matter of certainty that for the purpose of curbing terrorism and other crimes, information regarding tenants and other temporary residents shall be obtained, a database of such persons shall be prepared, such information shall be utilized for investigation and operations, and arrangements shall be made for related matters;

And whereas the session of the Provincial Assembly of Punjab has not been held and the Governor of Punjab is satisfied that circumstances are such that immediate action should be taken;

Therefore, I, the Governor of Punjab, in exercise of the powers conferred upon me under Article 128, sub-section (1) of the Constitution of the Islamic Republic of Pakistan, do hereby enact and enforce the following Ordinance:-

1- Short Title, Scope and Commencement:-
(1) This Ordinance may be called the "Ordinance for Information of Temporary Residents Punjab 2015".
(2) It shall apply to Punjab.
(3) It shall come into force immediately.

2- Definitions:- In this Ordinance:
(a) "Evidence" means the Evidence Act (1898);
(b) "Government" means the Government of Punjab;
(c) "Resident" means a person who is staying in any residence or who is staying in any hotel or hostel or guest house or lodging house, except for a hotel or hostel;
(d) "Hostel" means a place where accommodation is provided on rent or on any other agreement, but it shall not be a place for rent or a hotel, which is reserved for any organization or institution in which students or female students provided by that organization or institution are staying;
(e) "Hotel" means a place which provides accommodation to guests on rent or on any other basis, and it includes guest houses, resorts, motels, lodging houses or inns, but it shall not include a place for rent or a hostel;
