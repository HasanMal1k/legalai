3- Information to Police :- (1) Within forty-eight hours of the handover of the place of the Pani Patti Dealer, Zaidyar, and Tenant under sub-section (3), information regarding the tenant shall be provided to the police in a prescribed manner through the fastest means.

(2) The owner or Thier shall, within three hours of the arrival of a guest, provide information regarding the guest to the police in a prescribed manner through the fastest means.
