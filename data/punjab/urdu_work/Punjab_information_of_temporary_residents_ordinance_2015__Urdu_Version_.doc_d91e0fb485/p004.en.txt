5- Identification of Guests and Tenants :- (1) The owner, manager, or caretaker shall not allow any tenant or guest to stay in a rented house or hall unless they are satisfied with the identity of the tenant or guest on the basis of their computerized National Identity Card (CNIC) or passport.
(2) The owner, manager, or caretaker shall obtain a copy of the computerized National Identity Card (CNIC) or passport of the tenant or guest and shall provide such copy to the police as soon as possible.

6- Use of Information :- (1) The police shall maintain a database of all such tenants and guests in the prescribed manner.
(2) The police shall analyze this data for the purpose of community policing, intelligence gathering, and investigation.
(3) Information obtained under this Ordinance shall not be used for any purpose other than the prevention, intelligence gathering, investigation, and prosecution of terrorist crimes and other crimes under the Pakistan Penal Code, 1860 (45 of 1860).

7- Inspection and Interviews :- (1) A police officer, not below the rank of Sub-Inspector, shall have the power for the purposes of this Ordinance:
(a) to inspect rented houses or halls, and
(b) to obtain necessary information from various shops and persons including owners, tenants, managers, guests, or caretakers.
(2) The police shall ensure the implementation of this Ordinance with due regard to the situation and necessary measures.

8- Firearms and Explosive Materials :- (1) The owner, manager, or caretaker shall ensure that the tenant or guest does not possess any firearms or any explosive materials other than those authorized by a license.
