9- Non-Cooperation: The Government may, in the public interest, under this Ordinance, provide any non-cooperation to the police for the provision of information regarding the occupants, rental buildings, hostel or hotel libraries.

10- Assistance to Police: After the appointment of the Police Officer, the Police Officer shall be entitled to call upon any office, institution, or authority holding the record of ownership of a rental building or hostel, transfer deeds, or other title documents for the purpose of obtaining permanent information.

11- Penalties: If any person knowingly violates sections 3, 4, 5, or 6, or fails to provide access to the building or provide information to the police under section 7 or section 10, he shall be liable to imprisonment for up to six months and a fine ranging from ten thousand rupees to one lakh rupees.

12- Bailable and Non-Bailable Offences: (1) Offences under this Ordinance shall be bailable and non-bailable.
(2) Offences under this Ordinance of the first degree shall be tried by summary trials in accordance with the conditions of Chapter 22 of the Code of Criminal Procedure.

13- Settlement of Offence: (1) Subject to sub-section (2), the Government or, in this instance, the authorized officer of the Government, may settle an offence on the condition of payment of the amount of administrative fine under this Ordinance, provided it is not less than ten thousand rupees.
(2) An offence under this Ordinance shall not be settleable if the offence has already been punished or forfeited under this Ordinance, or if it has been settled by the Government or the authorized officer of the Government in a previous offence.

14- Addition to Other Laws: The provisions of this Ordinance shall be in addition to and shall not affect the meaning of any other law.
