(2) Subject to the provisions of Section 3, no person shall:
(a) use any external sound system in any place of worship only temporarily for the purpose of the Adhan, Jummah, or Eid prayers, or at the same rates as for the announcement of any ceremony, any person, or a day of mourning; or
(b) use an external sound system at any public place for a reasonable duration with the temporary permission of the Government or a designated officer on behalf of the Government, and the use of such sound system shall be subject to the conditions specified in the permission letter.

5- Inspection:- In order to ensure compliance with the provisions of the Ordinance, the Station House Officer (SHO) of the local police station shall regularly inspect the sound system of every place of worship at prescribed regular intervals, shall conduct such inspections, and shall complete the record of such inspections in the prescribed manner.

6- Punishment:- Any person violating any clause of Section 4 shall be liable to imprisonment for up to 6 months and a fine ranging from 25 thousand to one lakh rupees.

7- Power of Seizure:- (1) The police officer in charge of the local police station, who is not below the rank of Sub-Inspector, may seize any sound system which has been used in commission of an offense under this Ordinance or on which there is reasonable suspicion of being used in an offense based on the relevant incident.
(2) Any sound system seized under this Section, if not immediately destroyed, shall be produced before the competent court for the trial of the offense under this Ordinance.

8- Offense Cognizable by Police and Summary Trial:- (1) Any offense committed under this Ordinance shall be cognizable by the police and non-bailable.
(2) A Magistrate of the First Class shall conduct a summary trial of the offense committed under this Ordinance in accordance with the provisions relating to summary trials mentioned in Chapter 22 of the Code of Criminal Procedure.

9- Compounding of Offense:- (1) Subject to the provisions of Section (2), any officer, specifically authorized by the Government or on behalf of the Government, may compound an offense under this Ordinance at any level, provided it is an administrative offense and the fine is not less than five thousand rupees, subject to the condition of prevention.
