Whereas it is clear that for the purpose of preventing the promotion of activities of a subversive and disruptive nature in society, and for the sake of maintaining a conducive environment, public peace, and public order, and to prevent the commission of mischief or any other act, it is necessary that the use of certain sound systems in the Province be made regulated and lawful, and for the purpose of clarification regarding matters related thereto;

And since the Provincial Assembly of Punjab is not in session and the Governor of Punjab is satisfied that circumstances demand that immediate action be taken;

Therefore, the Governor of Punjab, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him under Article 128, sub-section (1) of the Constitution of the Islamic Republic of Pakistan, hereby makes and enforces the following Ordinance:-

1- Short Title, Scope, and Commencement:-
(1) This Ordinance shall be called the Ordinance (Regulation) Sound Systems Punjab 2015.
(2) It shall apply to the whole of Punjab.
(3) It shall come into force immediately.

2- Definitions:- In this Ordinance:
(a) "Area" means the United Provinces of India, 1898 (5th Act 1898);
(b) "Government" means the Government of Punjab;
(c) "Place of Worship" means a mosque, Imambargah, Dargah, temple, or any other place of worship of any sect or religion;
(d) "Prescribed" means as prescribed by means of rules made under this Ordinance;
(e) "Public Place" means a public seat, public road, public park, or a playground, or any other place to which members of the public or a section of the public have access by invitation or without invitation;
(f) "Sound System" means a loudspeaker, sound speaker, or any other device as may be specified; and
(g) "Proximity" means within a distance of two kilometers, that area or place where a sound system is placed.
