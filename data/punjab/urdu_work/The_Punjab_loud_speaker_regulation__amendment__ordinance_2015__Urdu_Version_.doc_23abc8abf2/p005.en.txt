10- Prohibition :- The court hearing an offense under this Ordinance may order the prohibition of any loudspeaker, sound system, or any other means of amplification used in the commission of an offense under this Ordinance.

11- Power to make rules :- The Government may, by notification in the official gazette, make rules for the implementation of the objectives of this Ordinance.

12- Enforcement and Exemption :- (1) Any offense committed in violation of the Loudspeaker, Sound System, and Public Address Regulation, Punjab, 1965 (2nd Amendment 1965) shall be punishable.
