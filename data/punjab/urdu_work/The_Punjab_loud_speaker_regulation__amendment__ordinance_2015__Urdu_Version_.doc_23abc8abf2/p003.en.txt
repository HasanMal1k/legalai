3- Prohibition on the use of sound systems :- (1) No person shall be permitted under this Ordinance to use, facilitate the use of, or permit the use of their sound system, whereby any unnecessary, non-essential, or unpleasant noise is produced, or whereby sounds from nearby persons cause restlessness, nuisance, discomfort, or disturbance to their rest, peace, health, safety, or security.
(2) Notwithstanding anything contained in any other law of this Ordinance, no person shall use a sound system whereby the prescribed limits are violated.
(3) The Government shall determine the limits of sound systems by means of notification.

4- Regulated use of sound systems :- (1) Subject to the following sub-section (2), no person shall cause the operation or use of a sound system:
(a) In a public place in such a manner as to cause noise or create noise whereby it causes discomfort and nuisance to the general public nearby, or --
(b) In any place in the vicinity --
(i) During prayer in a place of worship;
(ii) In a hospital providing facilities for the treatment of patients at any time during the day;
(iii) In educational institutions, courts, hospitals not providing facilities for the treatment of patients, or any other public institution, office, or place of business during working hours, in such a volume or in such a manner that the use of the sound system disrupts the routine of work or establishment, --
(iv) In a house or any other place being used for human residence at any time, unless specifically stated, --
(j) In any place of worship in such a manner and at such a volume that the sound emanating from the sound system can be heard at a distance of ten times the limits of that place of worship, or
(d) From any public or private place, broadcasting sounds of sectarian or other antagonistic nature, which may cause mischief in society, and such sounds can be heard from outside the limits of those places or from a distance or can be heard by humans.
