And since the Provincial Assembly of Punjab has not met and the Governor of Punjab is informed that circumstances demand that immediate action be taken;

Therefore, the Governor of Punjab, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him under Article 128(1) of the Constitution of the Islamic Republic of Pakistan, does hereby enact and promulgate the following Ordinance:

1. Short Title and Commencement:— (1) It is desired that this Ordinance be named the "Prohibition of Expression of Opinion on Walls Ordinance (Amendment) Punjab 2015".
(2) This Ordinance shall come into force immediately.

2. Amendment in Section 2 of Ordinance No. 2 of 1995:— In the Ordinance for Prohibition of Expression of Opinion on Walls Punjab 1995 (Ordinance of 1995), in Section 2 of the said Ordinance, the words "fine up to 5,000 rupees" shall be replaced by the words "fine shall be up to twenty-five thousand rupees".

3. Addition after Section 2 in Ordinance No. 2 of 1995:— In the said Ordinance, the following new section shall be added after Section 2:

"2. Police Arrestability and Summary Trial:— (1) Offence committed under the Prohibition of Expression of Opinion on Walls Ordinance shall be bailable and arrestable by the police.
(2) The provisions of Summary Trial as contained in Chapter 22 of the Code of Criminal Procedure, 1898 (Act of 1898) shall apply to offences committed under the Prohibition of Expression of Opinion on Walls Ordinance."

Dated: 8 January 2015

(Muhammad Sarwar)
