2. Definition of Shariat:
In this Act, by Shariat shall be meant the commands stated in the Quran and Sunnah or derived from them.
3. Supremacy of Shariat:
In the entire province of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, to the extent of the provincial jurisdiction, Shariat shall be the supreme law.
4. Interpretation of Laws:
All laws falling within the provincial jurisdiction in the province of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa shall be bound to be interpreted in accordance with Shariat.
Explanation:
(a) If there is scope for more than one interpretation in any other law, the courts shall be bound to adopt the interpretation that is in accordance with Shariat.
(b) If only two interpretations are possible in the interpretation of the aforementioned laws, the courts shall be bound to adopt the interpretation that is in accordance with Shariat.
5. Observance of Sharia commands and values by the government and citizens:
Every Muslim citizen in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa shall be bound by Sharia commands, and in this manner, legal regulations shall be formulated by the provincial government and its officials to make them bound by Sharia commands and observers of Sharia values. While making legislation, special care shall be taken that no law be contrary to Shariat or the Constitution of the Islamic Republic of Pakistan.
