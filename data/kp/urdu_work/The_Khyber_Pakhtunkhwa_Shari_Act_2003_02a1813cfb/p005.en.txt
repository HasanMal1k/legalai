(6) The Economic Reform Commission shall have the following functions:

(a) Recommendations for measures through which the economy shall be established on Islamic lines. These measures shall also include a viable alternative to the existing system.

(b) Identification of measures for the economic and social welfare of the people in accordance with the Constitution.

(c) Review of existing financial laws and laws regarding taxation, insurance, and banking from the perspective of whether the laws are in accordance with Sharia or in conflict with it, and if in conflict, proposals for making them in accordance with Sharia, etc.

(d) Proposals for the elimination of the system of interest (Riba) and identification of alternative arrangements.

(e) The Economic Reform Commission shall present its proposals to the Government, which shall be presented in the Assembly; all provincial government departments and officials shall be bound to cooperate with the Commission. The Finance Department shall be responsible for handling the administrative affairs of the Commission.

9. Propagation and Promotion of Islam through Mass Media:
Those mass media to which the provincial government has access shall be used for the purposes of Islamic propagation and promotion that are not contrary to the teachings of Islam.

10. Protection of Life, Property, and Personal Liberty:
The provincial government shall take necessary administrative and legal steps for the protection of the life, property, honor, dignity, and personal liberty of every citizen, which shall also include a ban on the display of weapons.

11. Elimination of Bribery:
The provincial government shall enact necessary legislation along with administrative orders for the elimination of bribery and corruption. We shall prescribe exemplary and severe discretionary punishments for the prevention of such crimes.
