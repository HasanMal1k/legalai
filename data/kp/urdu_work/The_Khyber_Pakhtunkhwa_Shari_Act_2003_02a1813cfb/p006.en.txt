12. Elimination of Social Evils:
The Provincial Government shall ensure *Amr bil Ma'roof* and *Nahi anil Munkar* in accordance with the command of the Holy Quran for the elimination of social evils from society, and shall provide for the same through administrative orders as well as necessary legislation.

13. Elimination of Indecency and Vagrancy:
The Provincial Government shall curb indecency and vagrancy through administrative orders as well as through legislation.

14. Reform of the Judicial System in Tribal Areas under the Administration of the Province:
(1) The Provincial Government shall appoint a Judicial Reform Commission within one month of the enforcement of the Indian Law, which shall consist of scholars, jurists, and legal experts, among whom one shall be its Chairman, and the Judicial Reform Commission shall submit recommendations on the following matters:
(a) Review of the prevailing law in the tribal areas under the administration of the province.
(b) Identification of defects in the existing law and proposal of alternatives.
(c) [unclear] Islamic Penal Code, Islamic Code of Criminal Procedure, and Islamic Law of Evidence.
(2) Upon receipt of the recommendations of the Judicial Reform Commission, the Provincial Government shall present them to the Governor of the Province for implementation in the tribal areas under the administration of the province.
(3) All departments and officers of the Provincial Government shall assist the Commission, and the Law Department of the Province of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa shall be responsible for managing the administrative affairs of the Commission.

15. Enabling Rules:
The Provincial Government shall make rules for the achievement of the objectives of this Act.
