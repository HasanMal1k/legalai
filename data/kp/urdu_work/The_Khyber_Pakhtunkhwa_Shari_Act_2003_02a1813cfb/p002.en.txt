THE KHYBER PAKHTUNKHWA SHARI ACT, 2003.

(KHYBER PAKHTUNKHWA ACT NO. II OF 2003)

[First published after having received the assent of the Governor of the Khyber Pakhtunkhwa in the Gazette of the Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. (Extraordinary, dated the 19th June, 2003).

Preamble:

Whereas it is that the sovereignty of the entire universe belongs to Allah Almighty, and the powers delegated to the elected representatives of the people in this God-given country of Pakistan, including the province of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, are a sacred trust;

Whereas it is that the objectives of the resolution have been incorporated into the Constitution of the Islamic Republic of Pakistan and have been declared a permanent part thereof;

Whereas it is that Islam has been declared the state religion of the God-given country of Pakistan. Therefore, it is obligatory for all Muslims to lead their lives in accordance with the Holy Quran and the Sunnah of the Prophet (PBUH) and to submit to the divine commands as individuals:

Whereas it is that a fundamental characteristic of an Islamic state is that it ensures the self-respect, life, liberty, and other fundamental rights of its citizens, which are guaranteed in the Constitution, and provides them with swift and immediate justice without any discrimination;

Whereas it is that the fabric of the Islamic way of life is based on the philosophy of non-tolerance of religious intolerance [unclear],

Whereas it is necessary for the achievement of the aforementioned objectives that constitutional and legal protection be provided to these measures.

Therefore, the following law is enacted.

Short Title, Extent:

1. (1) This Act shall be called the Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Shari Act 2003.
(2) It shall apply to the entire province of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa.
