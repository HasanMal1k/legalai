6. Education and Training of Shariat:
The Provincial Government shall take appropriate steps at the following levels for the education and training of Shariat:
(a) Inclusion of Islamic Jurisprudence (Fiqh) in the curriculum of all madrasas under the management of the Province.
(b) Education and attention to the Arabic language.

7. Implementation of Shariat in the Educational System:
(1) The Provincial Government shall ensure that its educational system guarantees the teaching of Shariat, Islamic character building, and Islamic training.
(2) Within one month of the enforcement of this Act, the Provincial Government shall appoint an Educational Commission. This commission shall consist of scholars, educational experts, jurists, and public representatives, and one of them shall be appointed as the Chairman of this commission.
(3) The Commission shall review the existing educational system and submit recommendations for the achievement of the objectives mentioned in sub-section (1) below.
(4) Upon receipt of the Commission's report, it shall be presented in the Provincial Assembly for necessary legislation.
(5) All institutions under the management of the Province, local governments, and administrative authorities shall be bound to cooperate with the Commission, and the Education Department of the Province of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa shall be responsible for the administrative affairs of the Commission.

8. Foundation of the Economy on Islamic Lines:
(1) To establish the economy on Islamic lines and for the achievement of the principles, objectives, and purposes of an Islamic economy, the Provincial Government shall appoint an Economic Reform Commission within one month of the enforcement of this Act. This commission shall consist of economists, bankers, jurists, and scholars, among whom one shall be its Chairman.
