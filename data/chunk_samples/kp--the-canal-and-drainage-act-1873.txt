The Canal and Drainage Act, 1873 — Overview
The Canal and Drainage Act, 1873 (Act, 1873, Federal).
This Act regulates the use of water for public purposes, irrigation, navigation, and drainage in the territories under the Pre-1947 Legislature. It establishes powers for canal officers to manage water supply, construct works, and recover charges, while defining liabilities for unauthorized use and damages. The Act also outlines procedures for compensation claims, labour requisitioning, and offences related to canal navigation.
Subjects: canals, irrigation, drainage, water supply, navigation, compensation, offences, rates and charges.
