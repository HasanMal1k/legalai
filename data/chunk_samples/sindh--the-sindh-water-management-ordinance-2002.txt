The Sindh Water Management Ordinance, 2002 — Overview
The Sindh Water Management Ordinance, 2002 (Ordinance, 2002, Sindh).
This Ordinance establishes a long-term, sustainable, and participatory framework for managing irrigation water, drainage, and flood waters in the Province of Sindh. It creates new bodies including the Sindh Irrigation and Drainage Authority (SIDA), Area Water Boards (AWBs), and Flood Offices (FOs) to replace previous structures. The law outlines their composition, functions, financial management, and powers regarding water allocation and enforcement.
Subjects: water management, irrigation, drainage, flood control, Area Water Boards, Water Users Associations, regulatory authority, financial management.
