The Companies Profits (Workers Participation) Act, 1968 — Overview
The Companies Profits (Workers Participation) Act, 1968 (Act, 1968, Federal).
The Companies Profits (Workers Participation) Act, 1968 enables workers in companies across Pakistan to participate in the company's profits. It mandates the establishment of a Workers Participation Fund and a Board of Trustees comprising worker representatives, management officials, and a Central Government nominee. The Act outlines eligibility criteria for workers, fiscal concessions for companies, tax exemptions for fund income, and penalties for non-compliance with profit-sharing provisions.
Subjects: workers participation, company profits, trusteeship, fund management, tax exemptions, central government rules, employee contributions, penalties for non-compliance.
Provisions: 3. Establishinent of Fund; 5. Penalty; 7. Act to override other laws
