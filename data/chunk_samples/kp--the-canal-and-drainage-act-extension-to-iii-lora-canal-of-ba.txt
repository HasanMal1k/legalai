The Canal and Drainage Act, (Extension to III Lora Canal of Bannu District) Ordinance, 1969 — Overview
The Canal and Drainage Act, (Extension to III Lora Canal of Bannu District) Ordinance, 1969 (Ordinance, 1969, West Pakistan).
This Ordinance applies the provisions of the Canal and Drainage Act, 1873 to the III Lora Canal of Bannu District. It validates the levy of occupier's rates collected since November 23, 1959, under previous notifications. The Ordinance also ensures that these levies cannot be challenged in court on the grounds that the 1873 Act was not applicable at the time.
Subjects: canals, drainage, irrigation, occupier's rates, Bannu District, III Lora Canal, validation of levies, application of acts.
