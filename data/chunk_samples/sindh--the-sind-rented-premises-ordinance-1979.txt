The Sind Rented Premises Ordinance, 1979 — Overview
The Sind Rented Premises Ordinance, 1979 (Ordinance, 1979, Sindh).
This Ordinance regulates the relationship between landlords and tenants within urban areas of Sindh to protect their respective interests. It empowers the Government to appoint Controllers who determine fair rents and oversee tenancy agreements, repairs, and eviction procedures. The law limits rent increases to ten percent per annum and repeals the earlier Sind Urban Rent Restriction Ordinance, 1959.
Subjects: rent control, landlord-tenant relations, fair rent determination, eviction procedures, urban areas, tenancy agreements, Controller authority, repeal of previous ordinances.
Provisions: 23. Judicial Proceedings; 27. Repealed
