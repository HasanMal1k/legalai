The Canal and Drainage Act (Extension to III Lora Canal of Bannu District) Ordinance, 1969 — Overview
The Canal and Drainage Act (Extension to III Lora Canal of Bannu District) Ordinance, 1969 (Ordinance, 1969, West Pakistan).
This Ordinance applies the provisions of the Canal and Drainage Act, 1873 to the III Lora Canal in Bannu District. It deems notifications issued since November 23, 1959 regarding occupier's rates as valid under the 1873 Act. Consequently, the Punjab Minor Canals Act, 1905 ceases to apply to this specific canal and area.
Subjects: canals, drainage, occupier's rates, irrigation, Bannu District, III Lora Canal, validation of levies, legal continuity.
