The North-West Border Military Police Act, 1904 — Overview
The North-West Border Military Police Act, 1904 (Act, 1904, Federal).
This Act regulates the Border Military Police Force in the North-West Frontier Province. It defines the force's constitution, appointment of officers and men, superintendence, and conditions of service. The legislation also outlines various offences committed by members of the force and prescribes punishments ranging from fines to transportation for life.
Subjects: military police, North-West Frontier Province, officer appointment, service conditions, offences, punishment, resignation, discharge.
Provisions: 4. Constitution of Force, I; 12. Powers and duties of Military conferrable'and Police-officers
