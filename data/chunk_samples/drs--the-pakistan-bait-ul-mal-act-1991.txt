The Pakistan Bait-ul-Mal Act, 1991 — Overview
The Pakistan Bait-ul-Mal Act, 1991 (Act, 1992, Federal).
This Act establishes the Bait-ul-Mal fund to provide financial assistance to destitute and needy persons such as widows, orphans, and invalids. The fund is administered by a Board of Management appointed by the Federal Government. Sources of financing include tax receipts, grants, voluntary donations, and income from property sales. The Act also provides for tax deductions on donations made to the fund and outlines the purposes for which the money can be utilized, including medical treatment, education, and self-employment schemes.
Subjects: Bait-ul-Mal fund, financial assistance for needy persons, Board of Management, tax deductions for donations, medical treatment for indigent sick, educational stipends, self-employment schemes.
