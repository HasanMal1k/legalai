(5) The prescribed fee includes all other fees. However, in case of any increase in the prescribed fee, the applicant shall be bound to pay the additional fee.

(8) It is necessary to convert manual birth certificates into computerized registration certificates.
To convert previously issued manual birth certificates into computerized registration certificates, after verification of the medical operation and medical training certificate from the patient's card, it shall be issued with the approval of the relevant council's standards and committee. And a certificate for computerized birth registration shall be issued upon payment of a fee of five hundred (500) rupees.

(9) Procedure for Registration of Orphans:
In such cases where children are found to be orphans and there is no information regarding their parents, and they are in the custody of any institution, company, or a trustworthy person, then in such a case, after the legal appointment of a guardian, they shall be registered, in which the name of the guardian shall be entered along with other details.
