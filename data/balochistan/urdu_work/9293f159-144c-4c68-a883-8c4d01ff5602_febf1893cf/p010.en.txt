(4) In the case of registries, the prescribed fee for the registration of births and deaths shall not be collected, and the prescribed fee for registration shall be collected from the concerned parties as per the rules.

(5) The relevant Registrar shall regularly have the report of the issued certificate or death report submitted to the Chief Inspector along with the register.

(6) Penalty for report of false information in the register of births/deaths:
Any government servant or citizen who violates these rules or creates any kind of false information in the register of births/deaths, upon discovery, shall be liable to punishment by the officer of the relevant department or under any other law in which such an act is punishable, and this punishment shall be enforced with any modification in the prescribed section.

(7) Cancellation:
The registration of births and deaths of the deceased persons shall be cancelled.
