(3) Date of birth or residence, parentage, place of birth or any change in status shall not be changed unless there is a court decision.

(4) For the registration of birth or death, it shall be necessary that the applicant refers to the court and the court's decision shall be considered final.

(5) Information regarding any type of birth or death shall be provided to the relevant Chief Officer.

(6) Procedure for posting birth and death registrations by the public:
Any citizen shall submit a written application to the Registrar/Administrator for the registration of birth, residence, or death. The application for posting the registration shall be written on the prescribed Register Form (C) and submitted to the relevant office within the notice period. Every Registrar/Administrator shall issue a receipt for the collection of the prescribed fee, which is an amount of 500 rupees, after payment by any citizen, and shall issue the registration within the prescribed time.

(7) Issuance of Certificate / Copy and Report:
(1) If any citizen applies for obtaining a copy of the registration, the prescribed fee, which will be an amount of 100 rupees, shall be collected after receiving the application, and the Registrar/Administrator shall issue the copy along with the registration in the relevant office and register. The applicant shall also receive a copy of the receipt for the collection of the copy on the Register Copy (Form-B), and the fee amount shall be deposited in the relevant office's fund.
