Government of Balochistan
Department of Municipal Administration and Urban Development
Notification

(1) Number: A-30/NBT-A-17/1366, dated: 21 January 2021. By virtue of powers conferred under Section (3) of the Balochistan Local Government Act 2020, the Birth and Death Rules are hereby approved and issued for public information under Paragraph No. (1) of the Fifth Schedule of the Balochistan Local Government Act 2020.

Scope
Short Title, Application and Commencement

(i) The Birth and Death Rules shall apply to the Birth and Death Registration Rules 2020 of the Government of Balochistan Local Councils.
(ii) The Birth and Death Rules shall apply to all local municipal and urban development councils active throughout the province.
(iii) The Birth and Death Rules shall come into force from the date of publication.

(2) Definitions
Unless the context otherwise requires, the following words and expressions shall have the meanings assigned to them as follows:
(a) "Act" shall mean the Balochistan Local Government Act 2020.
(b) "Government" shall mean the Government of Balochistan.
(c) "Authority" shall mean the District Council and Registration Authority.
(d) "Applicant" means the father, mother, grandfather, grandmother, sister, brother, wife, husband, or any relative of the deceased/born.
(e) "District Council" shall mean the District Council within whose limits the local council is situated.
(f) "Form" shall mean the form which is attached to these Rules and which is explicitly mentioned in Clause No. 3.
(g) "Mayor/Chief Minister/Administrator" shall mean the Mayor/Chief Minister/Administrator of the District Local Council, and shall have the meaning assigned to it in the Balochistan Local Government Act 2020.
(h) "Registrar" shall mean the Medical Officer for the Mayor/Chief Minister Corporation - Chief Officer for the Medical Commissioner and Medical [unclear].
