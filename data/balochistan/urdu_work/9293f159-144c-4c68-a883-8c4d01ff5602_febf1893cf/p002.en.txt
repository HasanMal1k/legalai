Part Two
(4) Duties of the relevant Union Council to make registration effective:
(1) Under these sub-rules, every Union Council shall register the professional matters occurring among the residents within its limits.
(2) The Registrar of professional matters shall keep the registers of professional matters safely in the registration office along with other records prepared under these sub-rules and shall issue certified copies thereof on the form.
(3) The Registrar of professional matters shall be continuously included in the responsibilities of supervision of the records of the office and shall be responsible for providing the required bonus (archive) as may be required from there.
