(10) Registration of Birth of Children of Pakistanis Born Abroad:
The registration of the birth of a child of a Pakistani citizen born abroad shall be made in the Pakistani Consulate in that country in the following manner:
(1) The applicant shall register the birth in the Pakistani Consulate present in that country within sixty (60) days from the date of birth with verification.
(2) In case of more than sixty (60) days, the application shall be entered into the Consulate after verification of the application.
(3) After registration, a copy of the Pakistani Consulate registration shall be sent to Pakistan by the applicant.
(4) The applicant shall present the original passport with the verification attached to the Chief Branch Officer of the Consulate under whose jurisdiction it falls, and after being satisfied, the Chief Branch Officer shall give a written order to the Secretary that the Secretary shall enter it in the register and necessary supervision shall be ensured during the process.
