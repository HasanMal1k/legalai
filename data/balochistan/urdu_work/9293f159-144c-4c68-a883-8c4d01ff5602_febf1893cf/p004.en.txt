(9) The Registrar shall regularly submit a report of all deaths occurring within the jurisdiction to the Administrator in the relevant chapter every month, and shall submit it twice on Form (B).

(10) Procedure for Secondary Entry of Death:

(1) After twenty (20) years from the date of death, the applicant shall submit the following documents to the Administrator in the relevant chapter for secondary entry and shall declare the secondary entry in the secondary chapter:
(a) An application requesting secondary entry, submitted on Form (A).
(b) An affidavit verified on oath by the applicant and two witnesses.
(c) A certificate of death issued by the relevant local authority, the Union Council, or the Municipal Corporation, along with a copy of the CNIC of the deceased (if available) or any other document showing the date of death. In case of divorce, a certified copy of the application from the relevant court's chapter.
(2) Upon receipt of the application, the Administrator in the relevant chapter shall verify the report of death and shall make the entry in the register.
(3) After this verification, the Administrator in the relevant chapter shall, on this application, conduct an inquiry and, within ten days, shall make the entry in the relevant chapter. In case of non-availability of documents, the Administrator in the relevant chapter shall issue the order for entry after conducting an inquiry.
(4) For cases where the death occurred more than ten years ago and for which no entry exists in the relevant chapter, the applicant shall apply to the relevant Medical Officer/DHQ Hospital to obtain a death certificate. The Administrator in the relevant chapter shall, upon receiving the death certificate from the relevant Medical Officer/DHQ Hospital, issue the order for entry in the relevant chapter. After making the entry in the register, the Administrator shall also issue a certificate thereof.
(5) For entries occurring after twenty (20) years of death, it is necessary that the applicant shall approach the court for the entry and a declaration of the oath may also be required.
(6) All objections to the entry in the relevant chapter shall remain pending in the relevant court/appeal case; in the event of such [objections], the entry may be stayed.
(7) Fee for entry: [unclear].
(8) During the period of mutation, the fee for entry of death shall be [unclear] (100) rupees.
