(9) Procedure for the registration of deaths:

(1) For individuals aged between 60 to 70 years, and subsequently for those aged between 4 to 69 years, applications shall be filed as per the entries in (1), (2), and (3), so that if they possess a death certificate, the options for the deceased and the family of the deceased shall be presented in the computer, and in any case, an application for the approval of the registration of the options for the deceased shall be submitted after the death of the relevant family member.

(2) For registration occurring after 6 years of death, it shall be necessary that the applicant shall approach the court for the registration and the court's decision shall also be recorded.

(3) After fulfilling all legal requirements, the decision regarding the registration of the deceased shall be made by the relevant Registrar. In the event of any legal violation, an inquiry shall be conducted against them in accordance with the law.

(13) Fee for registration of deaths:

(1) During the prescribed period, the fee for the registration of deaths shall be [100] one hundred rupees, for which a regular receipt shall be issued.

(2) From sixty (60) days to six (6) years, the fee for the registration of deaths shall be [300] three hundred rupees.

(3) After a period of six (6) years, the fee for the registration of deaths shall be [800] eight hundred rupees.
