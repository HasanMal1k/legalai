[Balochistan (Suppression of Tribal Violence Act) (Extension to Tribal Areas) Ordinance, 1963]
[W.P. Ordinance, III of 1963]

[28 May 1963]

Whereas the Balochistan (Suppression of Tribal Violence Act), 1958, extends to the Tribal Areas, Dera Ismail Khan and Peshawar and Dera;

Preamble:-

The Constitution of the Islamic Republic of Pakistan provides that the Governor of West Pakistan, in his capacity as the Approving Authority, may, in the interest of public convenience, make any ordinance for the Tribal Areas or any part of the Tribal Areas, which may be situated in a Province:

And in the interest of public convenience, the Balochistan (Suppression of Tribal Violence Act), 1958, shall extend to the Tribal Areas of Dera Ismail Khan and Peshawar and Dera;

Now, therefore, the Governor of West Pakistan, in his capacity as the Approving Authority, is pleased to make the following Ordinance:

Short Title and Commencement:-

(1) This Ordinance may be called the [Balochistan (Suppression of Tribal Violence Act) (Extension to Tribal Areas) Ordinance, 1963].

(2) This Act shall extend to the Tribal Areas of Dera Ismail Khan and Peshawar and Dera.

(3) It shall come into force in the usual manner.
