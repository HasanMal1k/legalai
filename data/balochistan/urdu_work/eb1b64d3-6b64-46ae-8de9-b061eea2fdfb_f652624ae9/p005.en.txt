4-4. Dissolution of the Committee.

When the [Name of Project/Work] in Pakistan or other areas is completed by the Government, the Government shall, by an order to be published in the Official Gazette, dissolve the Committee, and [provide for] the preservation and protection of [assets] as may be written in the order.

(2) An order shall be made under sub-section (1) to determine the [disposal/distribution] of matters between the [parties] which may arise upon the dissolution of the Committee, including the distribution of assets and liabilities.

5- Rules.

1- The Government shall make rules for the implementation of the provisions of this Act.

(a) Works which the Committee has added to the [infrastructure/works] as per the provisions of Section 3-A.

(b) Conduct of business through the Committee.

(c) Retention of balances of unallotted amounts and their [adjustment/distribution].

(d) Completion of works with [contractors], [including] their [technical] approval and technical approval.
