Pakistan Memorial Balochistan Acquisition Act 1964

(W.P. Act XXIX of 1964)
(W.P. Act XXIX of 1964)

[20 April 1964]
An Act for the collection of funds for the Pakistan Memorial.

Preamble—
Whereas it is deemed expedient that funds be collected for the Pakistan Memorial.

1. Short title, extent and duration—
(1) This Act may be called the Pakistan Memorial Balochistan Acquisition Act 1964.
(2) It shall extend to the whole province of [Balochistan] subject to existing laws.
(3) It shall come into force in this manner.

Definitions—
In this Act, unless there is anything repugnant in the context, the following expressions shall have the meanings assigned to them herein respectively:
(a) "Acquisition", means the acquisition of the Pakistan Memorial or the acquisition which shall be applicable under sub-section [A].
(b) "Committee", means the Pakistan Memorial Committee which shall be constituted under sub-section A.

-------------------------------

This Act was passed by the Assembly of West Pakistan on 8 April 1964, and has been published in the West Pakistan Gazette (Extraordinary) on 20 April 1964, pages 1289-1290; for statement of objects and reasons see West Pakistan Gazette (Extraordinary) dated 12 March 1964, pages 767A-767B.
2. In substitution of West Pakistan Ordinance V of 1966.
