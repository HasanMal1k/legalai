5. Addition of West Pakistan Ordinance 1962 and 1.

(a) "Amount" means Pakistan Rupees or other amount as established under sub-section 2-B.
(b) "Government" means Government (Balochistan).

3. Taxes to be levied.

(1) A tax shall be levied and collected on all buildings mentioned herein for residential houses and rural areas.
(2) This tax shall be levied:
(a) In the case of residential houses, at the rate of five percent; and
(b) In the case of rural areas, at the rate of ten percent.

3. Method of levying and collecting tax.
The tax mentioned in the section shall be levied and collected, as far as possible, under the laws of this Act in accordance with the procedure as follows: the tax shall be paid in [Balochistan] as per the taxable amount under the Act 1952.

3. Period for which tax shall be levied.
(1) Tax shall be levied until the day 13 June 1939.
(2) Notwithstanding the fact that the tax on houses under sub-section 2-B of the Pakistan [Balochistan] Act 1963, as the tax shall be levied under this Act, as interpreted through this Ordinance, shall be payable, it shall be applicable after the date 1 July 1928, in the provincial capital [Balochistan].

3-A. Constitution and Status Committee's functions.
The Government shall, as soon as possible, constitute a committee which shall establish the Pakistan or other [Balochistan] Commission. It shall consist of the Commissioner, Lahore Division, as its Chairman, and other government and non-government members as appointed by the Government.
