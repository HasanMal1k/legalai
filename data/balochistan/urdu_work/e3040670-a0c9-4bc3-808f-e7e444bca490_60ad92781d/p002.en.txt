Code of Civil Procedure (West Pakistan Amendment) Act 1963
(West Pakistan Act 1963, Code of Civil Procedure)
[12 July 1963]

An Act for the amendment of the Code of Civil Procedure 1898, to be applied to the Province of West Pakistan.

Preamble:-
Whereas it is deemed expedient that it is necessary that the Code of Civil Procedure be amended, to be applied to the Province of West Pakistan, and for this purpose the following has been enacted.

The following is enacted:

1. Short title, extent and commencement:-
(1) This Act, the Code of Civil Procedure (West Pakistan Amendment) Act 1963, shall apply.
(2) Its extent shall be the whole of the Province of West Pakistan, except for the Northern Areas.
(3) It shall come into force forthwith.

2. Insertion of Section 544-A in Act 1898:-

The following provisions are included in the Code of Civil Procedure 1898 after Section 532, as follows:

544-A Payment of compensation to the heirs of the deceased, etc. Where a person is punished for any offence involving murder, hurt, robbery, dacoity, mischief or criminal trespass, the court shall, while the accused is in custody, order the person to pay compensation to the heirs of the deceased, or in other cases, to the person who has suffered bodily hurt or damage to property, as may be determined by the court; provided that the amount of compensation granted by the court under the provisions of the Code of Civil Procedure shall be deemed to be the amount of fine.

Where any person is ordered to pay compensation under any provision, and such person is exempted from any civil liability by such order, the amount paid under this provision for the said hurt or damage to property shall be kept in a fund for the purpose of paying compensation to the person for any subsequent civil proceedings.
