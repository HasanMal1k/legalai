Sindh Cooperative Societies (West Pakistan Amendment)
Ordinance 1965
(W.P. Ordinance VII of 1965)
(30 April 1965)

An Ordinance to further amend the Sindh Cooperative Societies Act, 1925.

Preamble:-
Whereas it is expedient to further amend the Sindh Cooperative Societies Act, 1925 (Act VII of 1925) in the manner as appears hereinafter;

And whereas the Provincial Assembly of West Pakistan is not in session and the Governor of West Pakistan is satisfied that circumstances and conditions exist which render legislation necessary;

Therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him under clause (1) of Article 97 of the Constitution, the Governor of West Pakistan is satisfied that he may make and promulgate the following Ordinance.

1. Short title and commencement:-
1- This Ordinance shall be called the "Sindh Cooperative Societies (West Pakistan Amendment) Ordinance, 1965".
2- It shall come into force immediately.

2. Amendment to the Preamble and Section 72-A of Sindh Act VII of 1925:-
In the Sindh Cooperative Societies Act, 1925 (Act VII of 1925), in the Preamble and Section 72-A, wherever the words "Sindh" occur, they shall be substituted by the words "Province of West Pakistan".

---------------------------
This Ordinance was promulgated by the Governor of West Pakistan on 27 April 1965, as approved by the Provincial Assembly of West Pakistan on 6 July 1965 under clause (3) of Article 97 of the Constitution of the Islamic Republic of Pakistan, 1962 and published in the West Pakistan Gazette (Extraordinary) on 6 July 1965, pages 3-4.
