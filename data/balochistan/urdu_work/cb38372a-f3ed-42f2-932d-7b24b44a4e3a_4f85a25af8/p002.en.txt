Balochistan National Volunteers Ordinance, 1965

(West Pakistan Ordinance No. XXVIII of 1965)

[25 September 1965]

The purpose of the Ordinance is to prescribe laws for the National Volunteers of the Province of Balochistan.

Preamble:-
Whereas it is expedient that the purpose of the Ordinance is to prescribe laws relating to National Volunteers in the Province of Balochistan;
And whereas the Provincial Assembly of West Pakistan is not in session, and in such circumstances, it is the pleasure of the Governor of West Pakistan that necessary legislation be made in this regard;
Therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred in Article 9 of the Constitution, by the pleasure of the Governor of West Pakistan, the following Ordinance be brought into force in this regard.

(1) Short Title, Extent and Operation:-
1- This Ordinance shall be the Balochistan National Volunteers Ordinance, 1965.
2- Save as otherwise provided, this Ordinance shall apply to the whole of the Province of Balochistan.
