The provisions of the Balochistan Code 1985 shall be repealed and substituted, and shall be deemed to have come into force from 01/10/1984.

(9) National Volunteer shall be deemed a public servant.
A National Volunteer shall be deemed a public servant within the meaning of Section 3 of the Pakistan Penal Code.

(10) Bar on legal proceedings.
No criminal proceeding or any other legal proceeding shall lie against any National Volunteer who has acted in good faith under this Code or under any rule made thereunder.

(11) Immunity from prosecution for offences.
No court under this Code shall have the power to prosecute any National Volunteer for any offence committed while performing his duty, provided that he has acted with intent and in good faith; except with the permission of the Deputy Commissioner.

(12) Power to make rules.
(1) The Government may make rules for the purpose of achieving the objectives of this Code.
(2) In particular, and without prejudice, the Government may make rules for the following purposes:
(a) Discipline, conduct, and rules of service or instructions for the management of the organization of National Volunteers.
(b) Rules regarding the uniform, equipment, clubs, etc., of National Volunteers.
(c) Prescribed duties as mentioned in Section (3).
(d) A prescribed procedure under which a National Volunteer may be called upon to perform duty.
(e) When a National Volunteer is assisting the Police Force or any other service (force) established under the law for National Volunteers, the procedure for controlling the National Volunteer at that time shall be determined by that Police Force or service.
(f) Procedure for the prescribed membership and fitness of National Volunteers.

(13) Repeals and savings.
(1) The following rules are hereby repealed:
(a) Sindh Home Guards Act, 1983.
(b) West Balochistan National Volunteers Act, 1939.
