(3) Formation of National Volunteers.
The Deputy Commissioner shall constitute a body for his district, consisting of certain persons, which shall be called National Volunteers.
2. The Deputy Commissioner shall regularly appoint the members of the National Volunteers, provided that certain persons are suitable for the same and serve voluntarily.
3. The Deputy Commissioner shall have the power to dissolve this body, and the persons appointed by the Deputy Commissioner shall perform its functions.

(4) Duties of National Volunteers.
National Volunteers shall perform certain responsibilities related to maintaining law and order, public protection, and duties related to natural disasters.

(5) Summons for Training or Duty.
In connection with performing duties under the Code, the Deputy Commissioner may, at any time and under a specific procedure, summon National Volunteers or members for training or duty through a general order or a special order.

(6) Summons for Assistance to Police Force.
Whenever the Deputy Commissioner summons the National Volunteers for the assistance of the Police Force, or any other service which may be prescribed in the law, in such case, its control shall be exercised by any concerned officer of the Police Force as the procedure for its implementation is prescribed, and in such case, they shall have the same powers and protections as a person of the Police Force or a recovery officer.

(7) Dismissal.
The Deputy Commissioner may, in any manner, dismiss any National Volunteer for disobedience of orders, failure to perform his duty, or any other reasonable cause (after giving him an opportunity to be heard).

(8) Punishment.
If any National Volunteer is negligent in duty under sub-section 5, or refuses to obey an order, or is given any other duty according to the law without any reasonable cause, then he shall be sentenced to imprisonment for a term of three months, or a fine of 50 rupees, or both.
