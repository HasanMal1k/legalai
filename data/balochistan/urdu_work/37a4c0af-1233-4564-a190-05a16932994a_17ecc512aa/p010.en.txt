Other Acts 18. (1) Nothing provided in this Act or the definitions in this Act shall be inconsistent with any other laws, but shall be in addition to them.

(2) Nothing in this Act shall affect the right of any person to remuneration and no change shall occur in the same, and the Government and any of its agencies shall not be held liable for any other responsibility under any other law.

Legal Liability 19. Nothing in any other law shall be construed as holding the Government liable for any wrong committed by any citizen under this Act, nor shall it be understood that any wrong committed by the Government or any agency of the Government shall be the responsibility of the Government.
