Regulations. 22. (1) The Government may, by notification in the official Gazette, make regulations for the purposes of this Act, particularly in respect of delegated powers for which such regulations are made, or any of the following matters:
(a) submission of applications to the designated officer;
(b) payment to the victims of terrorism or any member of their family;
(c) repair and construction of houses of victims of terrorism;
(d) compensation for loss of life or damage to property caused by terrorism;
(e) details of information between the designated officer and the Government regarding the incident of victims of terrorism and authentic information;
(f) rehabilitation of victims of terrorism and their families;
(g) medical treatment of victims of terrorism;
(h) publication of information under this Act;
(i) procedure for application, appeal, and decision under this Act; and
(j) maintenance of accounts.

Repeal and Savings. 23. (1) The Balochistan Victims of Terrorism (Relief and Rehabilitation) Ordinance, 2013 (Ordinance Number 1 of 2013) is hereby repealed.
(2) Notwithstanding that the Balochistan Victims of Terrorism (Relief and Rehabilitation) Ordinance, 2013 (Ordinance Number 1 of 2013) as given as a repealed Ordinance, any thing done, any order issued, any notification, any powers given to any person for any investigation or action, payment or any order issued which was issued or remains issued to the victims of terrorism under any condition of this Act, shall be [unclear].
Unless it is repealed, withdrawn, cancelled, changed, or altered in accordance with the conditions of this Act.
