(4) The designated officer shall, within three days of receiving information under sub-section (3), make the payment of the amount to the affected citizen, whether required as a specific payment, via cross check, demand draft, or bank check.

(5) The Government shall, on the basis of rules, conditions, and delegations, send forms or other details to the designated officers; and a communication link shall be established between the designated officers and the Government, utilizing specific means of communication, to ensure the speed and nature of communication.

7. Treatment of affected citizens.
(1) The designated officer shall ensure the provision of immediate medical treatment to the affected citizen of terrorism from a hospital running under the control or ownership of the Government, local administration, or any institution of the Government.

(2) If the designated officer is satisfied that the treatment of the affected citizen is permissible in any hospital, then the designated officer shall request the Government that the transfer of the required amount in respect of the immediate medical treatment of the affected citizen be initiated.

8. Rehabilitation of affected citizens.
(1) In addition to the payment of medical treatment and compensation for an affected citizen, the designated officer shall propose measures to the Government regarding the rehabilitation of the affected citizen, so that the rules of the matter may be necessary to be arranged.

(2) The measures under sub-section (1) include:
(a) Monthly allowances to the affected citizen for a specific period.
(b) Education or technical training for the affected citizen or the members of their family, at the expense of the Government; and-
(c) Continuous medical treatment for the affected citizen or the members of their family.

(3) If any proposal is received by the Government under section [unclear], it may accept the proposal presented by the designated officer in whole or in part.

9. Procedure for payments.
(1) All payments made to any hospital under an act in respect of the medical treatment of an affected citizen shall be made by the designated officer via a cross check, demand draft, bank check, or any other means to ensure payment.
