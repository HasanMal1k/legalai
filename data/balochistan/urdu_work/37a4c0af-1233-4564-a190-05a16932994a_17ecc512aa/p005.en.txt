(l) "Act of Terrorism" means any illegal act in which any explosive material, weapon, or any other means of force is used by any terrorist against a person or property, the objective of which is to achieve political, sectarian, religious, linguistic, or financial goals by using intimidation or threats against the public or any section of the public, the Government, the Federal Government, or any institution of the Government, including any suicide attack, sectarian violence, and any armed conflict between a terrorist and a law enforcement agency;

Chapter II
Rights of Affected Citizens

3. (1) Rights of Affected Citizens - Subject to anything contained in any other law, every citizen shall be entitled to be granted a minimum amount in the schedule in respect of physical and financial losses incurred in the event of an act of terrorism.

(2) The Government may, by way of notification in the official Gazette, amend the schedule so that the minimum payable amount for an affected citizen may be increased, or any other form of loss may be included therein, and the minimum amount in respect of such loss may be determined.

4. (1) Fund for Affected Citizens - The Government shall, by way of notification, establish a fund for affected citizens from the Provincial Consolidated Fund so that timely payments may be made to citizens who are victims of terrorism.

(2) The Government shall ensure the provision of sufficient funds in the fund for affected citizens in view of its duties and responsibilities under this Act.

5. Welfare - The Government shall, in accordance with the proposed procedure, issue prescribed directions to formulate a welfare plan for any section of citizens who are victims of terrorism and the members of their families, particularly with regard to the education of children depending on them, and shall make arrangements for the provision of public health facilities and employment for affected citizens and the members of their families.
