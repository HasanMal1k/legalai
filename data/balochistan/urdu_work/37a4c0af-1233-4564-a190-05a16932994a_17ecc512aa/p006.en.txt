Chapter III
Procedure for Institution and Restoration

6. (1) The designated officer shall, as soon as possible, inform the Government of the details of any terrorist incident and the citizens who fell victim to it, along with the investigation, within fifteen days of the occurrence of the terrorist incident.

(2) The details of the investigation regarding the terrorist incident shall include:
(a) The time, date, place, and nature of the terrorist incident, etc.;
(b) The explosive materials, any flammable substance, firearms, or means of force used in the terrorist incident, etc.;
(c) The objectives of the terrorist incident, the identity of the terrorists, etc.;
(d) The law enforcement agency that conducted the operation and the name of the terrorist against whom action was taken;
(e) The details of the affected citizen along with their name, and the details of the damage caused to their body and property;
(f) The details of the spouse and other immediate relatives of the affected citizen;
(g) The details of the rights of the victim of terrorism under sub-section 3;
(h) The provision of details regarding the additional or monthly compensation to be paid to the affected citizen, and the reasons for the recommendation to pay the same; and
(i) The complete health investigation report of the law enforcement agency.

(3) The Government shall, within seven days of receiving information from the designated officer, transfer the required compensation to the affected citizen into an account, informing the designated officer, whom the designated officer shall update.
