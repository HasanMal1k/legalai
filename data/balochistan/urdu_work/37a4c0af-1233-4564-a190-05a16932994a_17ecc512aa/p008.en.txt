(2) The designated officer shall maintain a detailed record of payments made to any hospital in respect of the medical treatment of affected citizens or the members of their families on the prescribed form, and shall also send a copy of this record to the Government.

10. Application. (1) Any person may submit an application to the designated officer with the claim:
(a) that they are an affected citizen entitled to compensation under a specific clause; and
(b) that any person who has been declared an affected citizen but is not an affected citizen shall not be entitled to any payment or part of a payment under a specific clause.
(2) If any application is submitted under sub-section (1), the designated officer shall, after conducting such investigations as may be necessary, decide on such application within fifteen days from the date of receipt of such application in the context of the facts, circumstances, and events related to the matter.

11. Equality of Treatment. (1) Any relief declared under the Ordinance shall be available to all affected citizens on an equal basis, and no discrimination shall be practiced in respect of such relief on the basis of religion, race, color, or gender.
(2) The designated officer shall not practice any discrimination in respect of any affected citizen on the basis of religion, race, color, or gender.

12. Appeal. (1) Any person aggrieved by any decision of the designated officer may appeal to any other officer to whom the Government has delegated such powers within three days of receiving the decision, and the decision rendered on such appeal shall be final.
(2) The person exercising the power of appeal shall provide an opportunity for a hearing and shall decide on such appeal within three days of the filing of the appeal by way of a verbal order.

13. Residual Powers of the Government. (1) The Government may, at any stage, declare any person as an affected citizen by providing reasons and may direct the designated officer to make payment to such affected citizen under the terms of mutual consent.
