Minimum Relief Amount
Type of Loss
Losses suffered by victims of terrorism.

1. Death: 1,000,000/-
2. Severance or loss of function of any limb; Severe injury: 500,000/-
3. Inability to perform work beyond the extent of the loss; Apparent injury: 100,000/-
4. (1) Complete construction and residence: 500,000/-
   (2) Construction of a portion: 100,000/-
5. (1) Complete construction and shop, garage, or business: 500,000/-
   (2) Construction of a portion; Administrative premises: 100,000/-
6. (a) Bus, truck, or any heavy vehicle (per unit loss): 1,000,000/-
   (b) Car, jeep, or other four-wheelers (per unit loss): 200,000/-
   (c) Motorcycle or scooter (per unit loss): 40,000/-
   (d) Three-wheeler, and (per unit loss): 20,000/-
   (e) [unclear]: 40,000/-
7. (a) Buffalo, cow, or horse and loss of animals: 80,000/- (for each)
   (b) Buffalo, goat, and donkey: 40,000/- (for each)
   (c) [unclear]: 10,000/- (for each)
