(2) The Commissioner and the designated officer shall be bound by the instructions of the Government, subject to the rules made under this Act.

14. (1) Within three years of any payment made under this Act, if the designated officer or the Commissioner has any objection that any payment made under this Act has been made to any person on the basis of fraud or misunderstanding, the designated officer or the Commissioner may, in addition to any action taken under the law, recover the amount from that person who is not entitled to receive the amount, provided that the person shall be given an opportunity of being heard.

(2) Any person aggrieved by any decision of the Commissioner under sub-section (1) may file an appeal to the Government within three days of the date of the decision, and the decision in that regard shall also be [unclear].

(3) In the context of the appeal to be made under sub-section (2), the Government shall provide an opportunity of being heard to any interested person and shall pass a decision on the appeal within three days of the date of filing the appeal by way of a written order.

Chapter IV
Miscellaneous

15. (1) Assistance of Officers. The Government, local government bodies, and all officers shall be bound to assist the Commissioner and the designated officer in the performance of matters under this Act.

(2) During the performance of their matters under this Act, the Commissioner or any designated officer may request any information from the Government or any agency of the local government, and that agency shall be bound to provide such information.

16. Publication of the Law. The Government shall run publicity campaigns in electronic, TV, radio, and print media at intervals for the awareness of rights and the resolution of difficulties under this Act.

17. (1) Performance Audit. The Government shall conduct or get conducted a performance audit of the services provided and the payments made under this Act at intervals.
