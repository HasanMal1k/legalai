**Victims of Terrorism in Balochistan (Relief and Rehabilitation) Act, 2014**
**Act No. IV of 2014**

**ACT**
To provide for the establishment of an institutional framework for addressing the difficulties and hardships faced by victims of terrorism and their families, to provide immediate relief and recognition to citizens affected by incidents of terrorism, to recognize the right of victims of terrorism to relief, public assistance, and rehabilitation, to declare that they are entitled to state assistance, to arrange for appropriate funds for such assistance, to conduct analysis, research, and investigation regarding the losses suffered by citizens in incidents of terrorism, and to provide for the formation of a commission and incidental matters.

**Preamble**
Whereas it is necessary to take necessary measures for providing relief and rehabilitation to victims of terrorism and their families and for providing an institutional framework for the same, and for other related and incidental matters:

**Shall be implemented in the following manner:**

**Chapter I**
**Preliminary**

**Short Title.** 1. (1) This Act shall be called the Victims of Terrorism in Balochistan (Relief and Rehabilitation) Act, 2014.
(2) In addition, it shall apply to all tribal areas of Pakistan and to the province of Balochistan.
(3) It shall come into force immediately and shall be deemed to have come into force from May 14, 2013.

**Definitions.** 2. In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires, words and expressions shall have the meanings assigned to them in the title and text.
