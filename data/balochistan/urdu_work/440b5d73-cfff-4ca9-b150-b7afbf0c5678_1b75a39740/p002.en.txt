Pakistan Penal Code (West Pakistan Amendment) Act 1963
(W.P. - Act IV of 1963)

[18 April 1963]
This Act to be read as part of the Pakistan Penal Code in the Province of West Pakistan.
Preamble:-
Whereas it is expedient to make certain amendments in the Pakistan Penal Code in the Province of West Pakistan:
1. Short title.
(1) This Act may be called the Pakistan Penal Code (West Pakistan Amendment) Act 1963.
2. Amendment of Section A-302, Act XLV-1870.
In Section A-302 of the Pakistan Penal Code, the words "for two years, or afterwards, or both" shall be substituted by the words "for life imprisonment and also to beလား" [unclear].
