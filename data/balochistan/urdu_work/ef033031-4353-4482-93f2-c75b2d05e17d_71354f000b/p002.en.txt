West Pakistan Foodstuffs (Control) Act, 1958
(Application in Tribal Areas)

Regulation 1971

(Balochistan Regulation No. [unclear] of 1971)

[July 1971]

Preamble:-
A regulation for the application of the West Pakistan Foodstuffs (Control) Act, 1958, in certain tribal areas.

Whereas it is expedient that the provisions of the West Pakistan Foodstuffs (Control) Act, 1958 (West Pakistan Act No. 20 of 1958) shall be applied in certain tribal areas of the Province of Balochistan, including the provisions of the law and the orders and notifications issued thereunder;

Therefore, the officers, in exercise of the powers conferred upon them by the Board of Commissioners (B.C.) vide notification dated 25 March 1969, and while maintaining the powers conferred therein, do hereby enforce the following law/regulation as follows:-

(1) Short Title and Extent -

1. This law/regulation shall apply to the West Pakistan Foodstuffs (Control) Act (Application in Tribal Areas) Regulation, 1971.
2. It shall also extend to the following tribal areas of the Province of Balochistan:

(a) District Chagai, Tehsil Waland, including Mahri, Najrani, Qabila (County) and
(b) District Thob, Tehsil Fort Sandeman, Tehsil Hanurbagh and Tehsil Qila Saifullah.

3. It shall come into force immediately.
