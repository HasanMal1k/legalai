1. This law/regulation was published by the Balochistan side on 17 June 1969, 15 September 1980, and Balochistan Gazette No. 20 dated 21 July 1962. It was reserved in Article 381 of the Provisional Constitution of the Islamic Republic of Pakistan 1973, and its ratification was made in Federal Act 63 of 1975.

2. Wherever the word "Balochistan" appears in the law/regulation, it shall be replaced, and in its place, the word 'Pukhtun' [or 'Pashtun'] shall be used instead of 'Baloch'.
