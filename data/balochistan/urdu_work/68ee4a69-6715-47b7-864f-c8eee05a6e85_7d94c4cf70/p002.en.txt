Balochistan Financial (Amendment) Ordinance, 1981
(Of Ordinance XIII of 1981)

[25 October 1981]

An Ordinance to amend the Balochistan Financial Ordinance, 1981.

PREAMBLE:-
Whereas it is deemed expedient to amend the Balochistan Financial Ordinance, 1981 (IX of 1981) in the manner as appears hereunder;

And whereas the Governor is satisfied that circumstances exist which render it necessary to do so;

Now, therefore, the Governor of Balochistan, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him under Article 123 of the Constitution, which are conferred by Article 2 of the Constitution of the Islamic Republic of Pakistan, 1973, does hereby make and promulgate the following Ordinance:

1. Short Title and Commencement:-
1. This Ordinance shall be called the Balochistan Financial (Amendment) Ordinance, 1981.
2. It shall come into force immediately and shall be in force from July 1, 1981.
3. Amendment of Section 9 of Ordinance IX of 1981:-

In Section 9 of the Ordinance, 1981 (IX), (wherever the said Ordinance is referred to), after the sub-section (2) mentioned therein, the following new sub-section shall be inserted, namely:

(3) This Ordinance shall apply to all areas of Balochistan subject to the laws.

4. Substitution of Section 9 of Ordinance IX of 1981:-

In Section 9 of the Ordinance, the following shall be substituted, namely:

1. This Ordinance was issued by the Governor of Balochistan on 13 October 1981, published in the Balochistan Gazette (Extraordinary) No. 212 dated 25 October 1981, and is reserved and signed under Article 270-A of the Constitution of the Islamic Republic of Pakistan, 1973.
